Kant & von Rohr (2016) flame-spallation threshold — reference data
==================================================================

Source: Kant, M.A. and von Rohr, P.R. (2016), "Minimal required boundary
conditions for the thermal spallation process of granitic rocks",
International Journal of Rock Mechanics & Mining Sciences 84, 177-186.

This file contains only the data and thresholds needed to reproduce the
ALAMO MMWSpalling Kant validation test (step 15 in the implementation
plan). Test placement, physics enablement, and pass criteria are
documented in the plan, not here.

Key fact: this is a flame-jet experiment (methane-air burner impinging
orthogonally on granite), not an MMW experiment. The MMW beam is disabled
for this test; the heated patch on the top face uses a convective Robin BC

    q = h_fl * (T_flame - T_surface)

with (T_flame, h_fl) tabulated by stand-off distance below.


1. Material constants (constant, room-temperature; Kant Table 2)
================================================================

Bethel granite (Vermont, USA)
    lambda (k) = 2.62   W/(m K)
    cp         = 784    J/(kg K)
    rho        = 2566   kg/m^3
    kappa      = 1.3e-6 m^2/s
    E          = 58     GPa
    UCS        = 152    MPa

Gotthard granite (Gurtnellen, Switzerland)
    lambda (k) = 3.27   W/(m K)
    cp         = 792    J/(kg K)
    rho        = 2594   kg/m^3
    kappa      = 1.6e-6 m^2/s
    E          = 54     GPa
    UCS        = 214    MPa

Poisson's ratio nu: not reported by Kant. Assume nu = 0.25 for baseline,
or sweep nu in [0.20, 0.30] as a sensitivity study.

Initial rock T: 293.15 K unless otherwise specified.


2. Geometry and BCs
===================

Sample: 250 x 250 x 150 mm block (large enough that side/bottom BCs do
not affect the central early-time response).
Heated patch on top face: centred circular patch.
    Baseline diameter: 30 mm.
    Sensitivity sweep at one SOD: 20 / 30 / 40 mm
    (Kant does not give a complete spatial heat-flux distribution; treat
    the patch diameter as a robustness check, not a tuned parameter.)

Side + bottom thermal: adiabatic.
Mechanical: minimal bottom support (e.g. roller) to remove rigid-body
modes; avoid artificial confinement.

Heated-patch BC:
    q = h_fl * (T_flame - T_surface)


3. Table 4: SOD-indexed flame BC parameters
============================================

    SOD [mm]   q_sens [kW/m^2]   theta_s,sens [degC]   T_flame [degC]   h_fl [W/(m^2 K)]
    50         720               511                   1450             766
    80         866               552                   1410             1009
    110        755               495                   1360             873
    130        609               435                   1250             747
    150        450               389                   1100             633

Note: h_fl was measured indirectly via a heat-flux sensor plate replacing
the rock under the same burner settings. Sensor-calibration-dominated
uncertainty is ~17%.

Equivalent temperatures in K (T[K] = T[degC] + 273.15):
    SOD 50:  T_flame = 1723.15 K
    SOD 80:  T_flame = 1683.15 K
    SOD 110: T_flame = 1633.15 K
    SOD 130: T_flame = 1523.15 K
    SOD 150: T_flame = 1373.15 K

For SOD = 140 and 160 (used in the spall/no-spall outcome table below)
Kant does not tabulate (T_flame, h_fl). Interpolate linearly between the
tabulated SODs, or use the boundary values (SOD 130 / SOD 150) for
threshold testing.


4. Spall / no-spall experimental outcomes
=========================================

Each (SOD, rock) combination tested with three samples.

    SOD [mm]   Gotthard      Bethel
    50         spall         spall
    80         spall         spall
    110        spall         spall
    130        spall         spall
    140        no spall      spall
    150        no spall      spall
    160        no spall      no spall

Transitions:
    Gotthard transitions spall -> no-spall between SOD = 130 and 140 mm.
    Bethel   transitions spall -> no-spall between SOD = 150 and 160 mm.
Bethel remains spallable at larger SODs than Gotthard.


5. Threshold values
===================

Mean spallation surface temperature theta_SP:           ~600 degC
Mean failure-plane temperature theta_FP:                ~550 degC
Temperature drop on single spall detachment:            ~100 degC over a
                                                        few milliseconds
Minimal required surface temperature theta_SP,min:      ~500 degC (+/- 50)
Minimal required heat-transfer coefficient h_fl,min:    ~500 W/(m^2 K)
                                                        (+/- 100)
Upper bound (granitic melting at atmospheric pressure): ~1000 degC

Surface-temperature measurement uncertainty in the spallation range:
~5-6%.


6. Pyrometer emissivity (measurement-specific, NOT radiation BC)
================================================================

These are pyrometer emissivities at lambda = 2.3 um and viewing angle
30 deg. They are NOT the correct greybody emissivities for a simulation
radiation boundary condition.

    Gotthard:  epsilon = 0.549 +/- 12%
    Bethel:    epsilon = 0.434 +/- 17%


7. Caveats for exact reproduction
=================================

1. Stagnation-point heat-transfer coefficient only — no full spatial flux
   map.
2. Numerical model details (mesh, mechanical BCs) not given by Kant.
3. Poisson's ratio not reported (assume 0.25).
4. Pyrometer emissivity is measurement-specific.
5. Test threshold behaviour, not exact spall sequence.
6. A purely homogeneous continuum damage model may not reproduce the
   Bethel-vs-Gotthard ranking exactly without grain-scale heterogeneity
   (quartz/feldspar mismatch) or rock-specific damage parameters.
