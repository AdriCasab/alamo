**Scoring window:** from max(first removal + 10 s, 0.3 * t_end) to t_end, snapped to the 2 s plotfile times (first removal = first h_col CSV row). For runs too short for that (start later than 0.8 * t_end: the ~11 s M-* runs) the start is max(first removal + 2 s, 0.3 * t_end). ROP = level-set recession between window plotfiles; halves split at the plotfile nearest the midpoint. Top-cell T at firing = frozen T of all cells voided in the window (n = cells); exact top-cell sample when `1-cell` = yes (h_col CSV rows == voided cells, i.e. no event voided two cells). <dT_rem> = mean frozen H/(rho Cp) of the same cells. Flake = h_col of CSV rows in the window (first firing at each new top cell). T_s implied = T0 + (T_top - T0) x/(1 - e^-x), x = dz/delta, delta = kappa/ROP (surface-flux cases). Melt columns exclude the bottom 20 mm; `solid` = Lambda_L > 0 in a still-solid cell of any 2 s plotfile (first time, depth range below the mask top face); `removed` = voided cells whose frozen Lambda_L > 0.

## Discussion

Hand-written (2026-09-15, S1 implementer). The tables above regenerate; this
section does not. No parameter was tuned. All 35 runs close the whole-domain
energy ledger to ≤ 4.2e-14.

### What the criterion does in a resolved 1-D column

The single most important observation holds in **every run**:

- **Flakes are always sub-cell.** The logged h_col is 0.49·dz at 2 mm, falling
  to 0.22–0.26·dz at 0.0625 mm. h_col is proportional to dz: 0.98 mm at 2 mm,
  0.016 mm at 0.0625 mm.
- **Each event voids at most one cell.** `1-cell` = yes everywhere except
  M-100 at 0.125 mm.
- **The step fires at the same cell-average temperature.** The top solid cell
  reads **T ≈ 655–668 K (ΔT ≈ 362–375 K)** whatever the source, α, flux or dz.

This is the sp_weibull `top_cell` / `local_thermoelastic` criterion acting as
a **cell-average temperature threshold**:
- K_I for a 20 µm edge crack sees essentially the top-cell stress, so Sp = 1
  at a fixed top-cell T (with confining p = 1 MPa, scalar a0 = 20 µm).
- The K_I/K_Ic depth scan walks piecewise-constant cell temperatures and fails
  within the first stride. The sub-cell bisection then places h_col at a fixed
  fraction of dz.

As a result **flake thickness never converges**: it tracks dz with a −50% to
−72% change per halving. It is a mesh artefact of the criterion, not a
physical flake size.

**ROP follows from energy alone, as E1 found.** Every voided cell carries its
full enthalpy ρ·Cp·ΔT_top. Hence ROP = q_abs/(ρ·Cp·⟨ΔT_rem⟩); for example, F1
gives 2.06 MW/m² / (2.17 MJ/m³K · 363 K) = 9.4 m/h.

### Q1 — does the thermostat converge to a true-surface T_spall with sub-mm flakes, and at what dz?

**Partly.**

- **What converges at 2 mm.** The *resolved top-cell* T at firing is 655–660 K
  at every dz, drifting only +1% from 2 mm to 0.0625 mm (F1s, R-face). With
  the criterion as implemented this is converged at 2 mm, and so is fixed-flux
  ROP.
- **What does not converge.** It is not a true-surface T_spall. The implied
  true-surface T from the exponential ablation layer (δ = κ/v = 0.27 mm at
  9.3 m/h) is:
  - F1s: 3036 K at 2 mm, 1098 K at 0.5 mm and 704 K at 0.0625 mm, against a
    resolved 660 K.
  - R-face: 731 K at 0.0625 mm against a resolved 663 K.

  At coarse dz the true surface would be far hotter than the cell that the
  criterion reads. Implied and resolved values meet only once dz ≲ δ/4
  (≈ 0.06 mm at ~9 m/h, less at higher ROP).
- **Flakes:** sub-mm, but only because they are O(dz/2). The sequence never
  settles on a physical thickness.

**Answer:** the thermostat converges in its own (cell-average) variable at
2 mm, and in the true-surface sense only at dz ≈ 0.06 mm. Physical flake size
is not resolvable with the `top_cell` criterion at any dz tested.

### Q2 — converged Robin ROP vs the ~6 m/h hand number

Robin h = 1e4 W/m²K, T_gas = 1000 K:

- At the finest dz, cell and face bracket the answer:
  - R-face at 0.0625 mm: 3.13 MW/m², 14.0 m/h, rising.
  - R-cell at 0.125 mm: 4.15 MW/m², 18.6 m/h, falling.
- R-face increments shrink by ~0.55× per halving (+0.53, +0.29 MW/m²). A
  geometric extrapolation gives **q_abs ≈ 3.5 MW/m², ROP ≈ 15–16 m/h**, about
  2.5× the hand number.
- The hand estimate assumed T_s ≈ 812 K and ⟨ΔT_flake⟩ = 519 K; those came
  from the 2 mm Meier 2D harness (surface-normal kernel, Weibull flaws). Here
  the column fires at T_s ≈ 650–700 K with ⟨ΔT_rem⟩ ≈ 365 K. That raises the
  flame driving force h·(T_gas − T_s) and lowers the enthalpy per removed
  volume.
- So, with this criterion, the converged Robin ROP is set as much by the
  criterion's firing T as by (h, T_gas).
- At T_gas = 1400 K R-face is still far from converged (26 m/h at 0.125 mm,
  +75% on the last halving).
- As context point 5 of the packet warned: **no 2 mm Robin number means
  anything by itself.**

### Q3 — melt at M-2000 / M-100

**None.**

- No Λ_L > 0 in any solid cell of any plotfile, and no voided cell carries
  Λ_L > 0, in any case at any dz. This includes M-100: 15 MW/m², α = 100/m,
  a 10 mm absorption depth, i.e. volumetric deposition.
- Max T is the firing T itself: 655–674 K for voided cells at firing, and
  ≤ 668 K in the solid snapshots.
- At 68 m/h the ablation front outruns the volumetric pre-heating. In steady
  ablation each cell reaches the front with exactly the enthalpy it will carry
  away, so no subsurface peak forms above the surface T.
- The fusion onset (T_m − fus_width/2 = 1448 K) is never approached.

### Q4 — size and sign of the coarse-mesh error, R-cell vs R-face

Relative to the extrapolated converged ≈ 3.5 MW/m², 15.5 m/h, at dz = 2 mm
(Bi = h·dz/2k = 6.7):

| Form | q_abs | ROP | Error | Monotone with dz? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-cell | 4.98 MW/m² | 22.8 m/h | **+42% / +47% (over-absorbs)** | yes |
| R-face | 0.63 MW/m² | 2.8 m/h | **−82% (under-absorbs)** | yes |

- At 2 mm the two forms differ 8× in absorbed flux, matching the ~10× note
  estimate. Both errors shrink monotonically with dz, from opposite sides.
- **Cell form** over-absorbs because it applies h·(T_gas − T_c) with T_c below
  the true surface T.
- **Face form** under-absorbs because its linear face-to-centre profile
  (conductance 2k/dz) is far too weak when the real thermal layer
  δ = κ/v ≈ 0.1–0.3 mm is ≪ dz. The face runs hot: at 2 mm, T_gas = 1400 K,
  losses at the face reach 129 kW/m².
- The cell form is the *less wrong* one at 2 mm; neither is controlled.

### Recommendations

**(i) Sub-cell closure vs AMR + removal.**
- **Fixed flux:** no surface closure is needed for ROP or energy; 2 mm is
  within 1% of 0.0625 mm.
- **Robin (and radiation/melt, which read the true surface T):** a **sub-cell
  surface closure is required and should be sufficient**. Replace the face
  form's 2k/dz with the ablation-layer conductance implied by the cell
  average and v (δ = κ/v). Both uncorrected forms are off by 0.18× to 1.4× at
  2 mm, and the converged layer is 0.1–0.3 mm thick.
- **AMR + removal** would need ~0.06 mm at the surface to converge Robin ROP.
  Recommend it only if the closure fails an A/B against the 0.0625 mm columns
  here.
- Flake thickness is a *criterion* discretisation issue (the depth scan on
  piecewise-constant T). That is the "sub-cell criterion closure" step and is
  separate from the flux closure.

**(ii) P1 "melt unreachable":** **survives, and is strengthened.**
- No melt anywhere, including volumetric M-100 at 15 MW/m².
- Resolving the surface moves the firing T up by only +1–2%.
- The packet's corollary 4 is confirmed: resolution pins the true surface
  closer to T_spall, not above it.
- Melt needs either a much higher firing T (a different criterion or
  confinement) or a source that heats faster than the front can remove.
  Neither exists in this model.

**(iii) Robin form to carry forward:** **face** (`robin_form = face`) as the
discretisation, together with the sub-cell conductance closure from (i).
- It is the consistent finite-volume flux: it converges monotonically from
  below, has the right Bi → 0 limit, and already solves the nonlinear face
  balance with losses.
- It is the natural host for the closure.
- Do **not** use raw face at 2 mm, where it is −82% off. Until the closure
  exists, a 2 mm Robin run should be reported only as a (cell, face) band,
  roughly [0.2×, 1.4×] of the converged flux.

### Caveats

- **Geometry and flaws:** 1-D columns (no lateral conduction, no surface
  normal, no Weibull scatter; scalar a = a0 via a degenerate Weibull draw).
  The Meier 2D harness fires at a different ΔT (519 K), so the absolute ROPs
  here are not Meier predictions.
- **Stop times:** the packet's hand-ROP stop rule drilled through the column
  for R-cell, M-*, and the finest R-face / R-face-1400. Those cases use
  measured time-to-200-mm stops (`STOP_OVERRIDE` in `run_sweep.py`); shortening
  stop_time was verified to leave the earlier trajectory bit-identical.
- **Scoring windows:** snapped to 2 s plotfiles. The M-* windows are 4.0–10.6
  / 10.9 s: first removal + 2 s, because the runs are ~11 s.
- **`1-cell` flag:** "no" for M-100 at 0.125 mm (at least one event voided two cells),
  so its top-cell T sample includes a few sub-top cells.
- **Implied T_s:** assumes a steady exponential layer across the whole top
  cell. It is meaningless (thousands of K) when dz ≫ δ and is shown only to
  expose the gap.
