## Discussion

Hand-written 2026-09-15 (C1 implementer). No parameter was tuned; the tables
above regenerate and this section does not.
- **Ledger:** closes to ≤ 2.6e-13 in every run.
- **Time step:** dt obeys the overshoot rule everywhere (≤ 3.4 K/step).
- **ROP measure:** quote the **ROP fit** columns (least-squares slope of each
  column's cumulative `h_applied`, sub-cell). The plotfile ROP is a whole-cell
  staircase; for example, 2 mm over a 20 s window is ±6 %.

### Part 1: 1-D columns vs S1

**(i) 2 mm vs S1's bracket and the closed form.** P-1000 at dz = 2 mm gives
**ROP 15.67 m/h** with ⟨q_abs⟩ = 3.439 MW/m² and T_fire = 655.7 K.
- **Bracket:** inside S1's hard bracket 14.0–18.6 m/h, where R-face at
  0.0625 mm was still rising and R-cell at 0.125 mm still falling.
- **Closed form:** at the run's own T_fire the closed form gives 3.431 MW/m² /
  15.68 m/h, a ratio of **0.999**. The target was within 10 %.
- **Q2 extrapolation:** S1 estimated 3.5 MW/m² / 15–16 m/h; the closed form
  from 09-15b gives 3.39 / 15.3.
- **Coarse-mesh gap closed:** at 2 mm the closure removes S1's +42 % (cell) and
  −82 % (face) errors.

**(ii) Change per halving.** The target was < 5 %.

| dz step | ROP fit | q |
|---|---|---|
| 2 → 1 mm | −0.52 % | −0.23 % |
| 1 → 0.5 mm | −0.59 % | −0.29 % |
| 0.5 → 0.25 mm | +0.03 % | +0.02 % |

- The small residual drift follows T_fire, which rises +1.1 K from 2 to
  0.25 mm (655.7 → 656.8 K; the 20 µm scalar flaw has no volume factor).
- The closed form tracks the ROP: 15.68 → 15.59 m/h.
- ROP fit / ROP_cf = 0.999, 0.997, 0.993, 0.994.

**(iii) P-1400 (T_gas = 1400 K).**
- 2 mm: ROP fit **33.77 m/h** vs closed form 33.91 (0.996).
- 0.5 mm: **33.40** vs 33.60 (0.994).
- Change 2 → 0.5 mm: −1.1 %.
- Both lie above S1's unconverged R-face-1400 lower bound of 26 m/h, which was
  still rising +75 % per halving, and within 2 % of 09-15b's closed form of
  33.4 m/h at T_fire = 660 K.
- The plotfile ROP at 2 mm (33.15, halves 32.15 / 34.10) is staircase noise
  over the 4 s window.

**(iv) `pinned_idle_cycles` sensitivity at 2 mm.** idle = 1 and idle = 4 are
**bit-identical** to idle = 2: `removal_events.csv` and every Cell_D match, a
0.00 % ROP change.
- In the 1-D column a cell fires twice on consecutive steps, then the next
  cell fires after a gap of 0.459–0.460 s. The cell heating time is
  t_cell = ρ·Cp·dz·(T_pin − T_a)/q_pin = 0.458 s, so the gap is **1.004·t_cell**.
- With idle = 1 the pin could lapse only in the last ~2 ms before each
  refiring. At 2 mm it evidently never does (t_cell uses each firing's own
  pin_T), but that is a marginal result, not a robust insensitivity.
- In 2-D (M-10k-1000, 2 mm) the per-column firing gaps reach 1.70 s =
  **1.32·t_cell**, so idle = 1 would drop steady columns back to face there.
- The default of 2 has margin in both.

**(v) Analytic sensitivity.** d ln ROP/dT_fire = −1/(T_gas − T_f) − 1/(T_f − T_0):

- **The 1-D closed form holds at the centre of a 50 mm footprint in 2-D.**
- M-20k-1400's window is only 6–7.9 s (80 mm reached at 7.9 s). Its halves
  36.5 / 32.7 m/h span ~1 s each and are not a meaningful deceleration test.

**Does any case stall? Do ring columns fall back and recover?**
- **No stall anywhere in the footprint interior.** ROP-fit halves are steady:
  - M-5k-1200: 5.88 / 5.86;
  - M-10k-1000 at 2 mm: 5.58 / 5.50;
  - M-10k-1000 at 1 mm: 5.51 / 5.51.
- **One real, reportable stall at the patch rim, in M-10k-1000 only.** The
  outermost in-patch column pair never fires in 40 s:
  - 2 mm: x − x0 = ±23 mm, 8 of 96 footprint columns;
  - 1 mm: x − x0 = ±24.5 mm, 16 of 400.

  These columns never reach the pin, so this is the **face form before any
  firing**, not the min rule. Their face flux, which is weak at h = 1e4 with
  G = 2k/dz = 1500 W/m²K at 2 mm, is lost laterally to the unheated rock
  outside the patch. At h = 5e3 with T_gas = 1200 K, and at h = 2e4 with
  T_gas = 1400 K, every footprint column fires. The rim therefore has a sharp
  patch-edge artefact whose width is one cell at each mesh. D2's radial jet
  profile removes the sharp edge.
- **Recovery.** Every ring column that fired stays pinned: the reconstructed
  active-pin fraction is 1.000 for fired ring columns. The M-10k ring figure
  of 0.500 at 2 mm and 0.800 at 1 mm is entirely the never-fired rim pair.
- **Face share.** The thermo face fraction (1 − pinned frac) is 0.1 % for
  M-5k-1200, 0 % for M-20k, and 8.3 % / 4.0 % for M-10k at 2 / 1 mm, which
  equals the never-fired rim share. Transient min-rule or idle fallbacks in
  fired columns are therefore ≤ 0.1 % of in-patch cell-steps. In this uniform
  footprint the min rule rarely engages once a column has fired, because
  T_face stays above T_pin.

**Mesh check M-10k-1000, 2 → 1 mm** (5 % bar):
- T_fire: +0.02 % of ΔT_fire.
- **Centre ROP fit: 5.55 → 5.51 m/h (−0.7 %), PASS.**
- The plotfile centre ROP reads −6.1 %, but that is the 2 mm staircase

- The footprint-mean ROP fit reads +4.8 %. The rim pair that never fires
