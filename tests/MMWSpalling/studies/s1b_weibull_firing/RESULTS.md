## Discussion

Hand-written 2026-09-15 (A1 implementer). No parameter was tuned; the tables
above regenerate and this section does not. All 14 runs close the ledger at
≤ 1.1e-14.

**Sanity checks.**
- ⟨ΔT_rem⟩ equals T_fire − 293.15 K to within 0.3 K in every run, so every
  voided cell leaves at the firing temperature, as in S1.
- The E-check is 0.978–0.997, not exactly 1.00, for two reasons:
  - surface losses take 0.5–1.1 % of q (window-mean `ledger_P_loss`/`ledger_P_beam`:
    0.47 % at W-def 2 mm, 1.13 % at 0.25 mm);
  - the 2 s snapping of a whole-cell removal staircase adds up to ±2.6 % at
    2 mm (one 2 mm cell over a ~75 mm window). The W-cell 2 mm halves,
    6.11 / 6.54 m/h, show this.

### (i) Firing-temperature drift per halving: W-def vs W-cell

| dz step | W-def ΔT_fire | W-cell ΔT_fire | W-def ROP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 → 1 mm | **+10.7 K (+2.06 %)** | +1.6 K (+0.30 %) | 6.496 → 6.436 m/h (−0.9 %) |
| 1 → 0.5 mm | +9.2 K (+1.73 %) | +0.3 K (+0.07 %) | −2.3 % |
| 0.5 → 0.25 mm | +8.9 K (+1.66 %) | +0.0 K | −1.8 % |

- **W-def** drifts steadily at ~+9–11 K per halving and does not converge.
  Its vol_factor = (V_cell/V0)^(1/20) ∝ dz^(3/20) has no floor.
  - Mean a_f falls 4.39 → 3.90 → 3.51 → 3.15 µm (−11 % per halving, as the
    factor predicts).
  - ΔT_fire rises only ~2 % per halving, not the ≈ +5.3 % that a^(−1/2)
    would give. The rising K_Ic(T) branch damps it.
  - Over three halvings: +29 K on T_fire, −5.0 % on ROP (6.50 → 6.17 m/h).
- **W-cell** (V0 = dz³, vol_factor ≡ 1) is flat at 821.4–821.8 K from 1 mm
  down, and 819.8 K at 2 mm.
- **Against Packet D's 3 % mesh bar at 2 → 1 mm:** W-def passes on both
  T_fire (+2.06 % of ΔT) and ROP (−0.9 %), but the drift is systematic, not
  noise. A 2 → 0.5 mm check would show +3.8 % on ΔT and −3.2 % on ROP. W-cell
  passes with margin (+0.3 %).

### (ii) Does T_fire at 2 mm land at ≈ 812 K (ΔT ≈ 519 K) as on Meier 2D?

**Yes.**
- **Match to Meier 2D:** W-def 2 mm fires at **810.8 K** (p10–p90 805.5–816.9)
  with ⟨ΔT_rem⟩ = **517.8 K**, against Meier 2D's 812 K / 519 K. That is 1.2 K
  apart, well inside the 15 K bar.
- **The column reproduces the 2D harness's firing temperature**, so the
  column is a valid surrogate for T_fire.
- **ROP:** 6.50 m/h, against the 6.3 m/h 2D harness and the hand
  2.06e6/(2.1725e6·519) = 6.58 m/h.
- **Meier's differing keys** (`spall.surface_normal = 1`, `spall.melt_aware = 0`,
  `spall.flake_coherence_length = 0.004`) were run one at a time anyway. Each
  is **bit-identical** to W-def in this column (same T_fire, ROP and a_f to
  every printed digit):
  - a single column has no neighbours, so cos θ = 1 and there is no
    coherence cap;
  - no cell reaches the fusion onset, so melt_aware has no effect.

### (iii) Seed sensitivity

Seed 777 vs 12345:
- 2 mm: 811.4 vs 810.8 K (+0.6 K), ROP 6.502 vs 6.496 m/h;
- 0.5 mm: 831.3 vs 830.6 K (+0.7 K).

The mean T_fire is insensitive to the seed (< 1 K). The drift in (i) is
systematic, not a draw artefact. Note that each column refires on a fresh
cell as it recedes, so a 60 s window samples ~38–290 cells (76–582 firings, two per cell).

### (iv) Per-cell scatter vs the mean flaw (S-4p4 vs W-def at 2 mm)

S-4p4 uses a scalar a = 4.436 µm = a0_ig·vol_factor(2 mm), which is the draw
without the (−ln U)^(1/m) scatter. It fires at **809.7 K** (p10–p90
809.5–809.9) vs W-def's 810.8 K [805.5, 816.9].
- **Mean T_fire:** the scatter shifts it by **+1.1 K**. The drawn flaws
  average 4.386 µm, slightly below 4.436 µm (E[(−ln U)^(1/20)] = Γ(1.05) = 0.974 < 1),
  hence a marginally higher T.
- **Spread:** the scatter adds ~±6 K of firing-to-firing spread.
- **ROP** is unchanged (6.489 vs 6.496 m/h).
- **Conclusion:** the mean flaw sets T_fire; per-cell scatter is a
  second-order effect on the mean.

### (v) Weibull reference volume V0: options, with numbers (the user decides)

1. **Keep the volume scaling (V0 = 1e-9 m³, current default).**
   - *Interpretation:* a physical weakest-link size effect referenced to the
     cell.
   - *Cost:* T_fire and ROP depend on the mesh at ~+9–11 K (~2 % of ΔT) and
     ~−1 to −2 % ROP per halving, with no convergence. The cell volume is
     not the volume the surface criterion actually samples (a ~20 µm crack
     in the top cell).
   - *Calibration:* Meier-2D's 2 mm calibration stays as it is (811 K).
2. **V0 = V_cell per input (vol_factor ≡ 1; scatter only).**
   - *Numbers:* mesh-independent to ≤ 1.6 K (0.3 %) from 2 mm down; T_fire ≈
     821.6 K (mean a_f ≈ 3.9 µm = a0_ig·E[(−ln U)^(1/20)]). This equals
     W-def at the 1 mm reference, where V_cell = 1e-9.
   - *Cost:* a 2 mm Meier run would fire +9 K hotter than today (811 → 820 K,
     ΔT +1.7 %) and drill ~2.5 % slower (6.50 → 6.33 m/h, within the
     staircase noise).
   - *Interpretation:* `a0_ig` becomes "the flaw of the sampled cell",
     explicitly not size-scaled.
3. **A physical reference volume V_ref (a grain, or the heated layer).**
   - *Implementation:* replace V_cell by V_ref in vol_factor, a constant
     over the mesh. Numerically this is option 2 with a rescaled a0:
     a0_eff = a0_ig·(V_ref/V0)^(1/20).
   - *What it would need:* a defensible V_ref and a re-referenced a0_ig.
     - **Grain:** Grimsel grain size, e.g. (1–3 mm)³, gives factor 1.00–1.18,
       i.e. T_fire ≈ 822 K down to ~800 K.
     - **Heated skin:** δ·A with δ = κ/v ≈ 0.38 mm at 6.5 m/h. A would have
       to be a physical spall-patch area (not the cell area), otherwise it
       reintroduces mesh dependence; it also makes V_ref depend on ROP.
   - *Cost:* choosing V_ref amounts to calibrating the flaw size, which the
     no-calibration guardrail keeps out of this step.

**Recommendation:**
- **Option 2 (V0 = V_cell)** for Packet C/D. It is the only choice whose
  firing temperature is mesh-independent, which is what C's
  `q = h·(T_gas − T_fire)` closure and D's mesh check need. The shift from
  today's 2 mm value is +9 K / −2.5 % ROP.
- If a size effect is wanted physically, option 3 with a grain-scale V_ref
  is the principled version, but it needs a calibration decision first.
- Keep option 1 only if Packet D is run at a single fixed mesh.

Nothing in the Meier inputs was changed.
