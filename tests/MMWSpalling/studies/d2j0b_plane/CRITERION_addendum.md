discrete corner sink of the per-column model (a firing top cell beside a taller neighbour). The
only existing switch that changes how a column behaves at a step is `spall.surface_normal = 1`
(Step 20: slope-aware K_I scan and h/cos θ recession; D2b found it inert on the burner ROP under
the pinned closure). Runs: C1n_2mm, C0n_2mm, C1n_1mm = C1 / C0 with `spall.surface_normal=1`.

Prediction: **it does not remove the cascade.** The pinned flux and the lateral loss through the
step are unchanged by the kernel; it changes only how deep a firing column cuts per event.
Scored by the same band table: if C1n's Δ_outer at 1 mm stays within 2× of C1's (8–15 s) and the
1 mm/2 mm gap still grows block on block, the prediction holds and the switch is not a remedy.
If C1n shows no band stopping before its own crossing, the kernel regularises the corner and is
the cheapest candidate packet.
