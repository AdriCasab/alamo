# P1g-2b `(a5)` — the hole-wall coefficient: what is settled, what is not

Generated by `wall_findings.py`. Offline; no source edited, nothing run.

## ⚠⚠ The deferral, and its reason

**Wall heating is not the width-setting feedback, so the wall-jet law is
deferred.** The reason is the **brake**, not the lever:

- P2-0's physics case was a *feedback*: a duct coefficient rises steeply as
  the hole narrows, so the bore becomes an equilibrium instead of a runaway.
- In a **radial wall jet** that feedback nearly vanishes. Momentum is
  conserved over an expanding annulus, so `v_m ∝ 1/R` while `Re` stays
  roughly constant, hence `c_f` barely moves and `h ∝ 1/R` — a **linear**
  brake, against the duct's steep one.
- ⚠ The brake is **convention-independent**: changing the `c_f` definition is
  a constant multiplier, so every *ratio* survives it. This is the one
  wall-jet quantity that can be recorded as settled.

Measured on one diameter pair at a time, since a ratio needs a stated pair:

| diameter pair | duct brake (backed) | wall-jet brake `1/R` | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ø84 → Ø121.8 | **4.04×** | **1.45×** | 2.8 |
| Ø102 → Ø121.8 | **1.75×** | **1.19×** | 1.5 |
| Ø84 → Ø102.0 | **2.30×** | **1.21×** | 1.9 |

⚠ **The packet's "1.2× against the duct's 4.04×" compares two different
diameter pairs** — 4.04 is Ø84→Ø121.8 and 1.2 is Ø102→Ø121.8. On a common
pair the gap is 4.04 vs 1.45, or 1.75 vs 1.19. The conclusion is unchanged
either way and is **qualitative**: the duct brake is 2.8–3× the wall jet's.

## ✅ Settled: the wall is a jet, not a duct

The faces that actually take wall treatment sit inside an impinging/radial
wall jet, not in developed annular duct flow. The duct value is the
**far-field limit misapplied**:

- duct Gnielinski at Ø102.2 mm reproduces **50.3 W/m²K** (backed, from
  `annulus_h.py`), and the reviewer's note recovers it as the `z > z_fill`
  limit rather than contradicting it — it is simply not where the faces are.
- hydraulic diameter there is **35.2 mm**, so the live wall band sits a few
  `D_h` below the nozzle, inside the developing jet.

single definition is the largest lever in the whole estimate.

a rate-containment decision, not a side effect.

## What this note backs — and the four things not to quote

✅ **Backs:** the wall is a jet not a duct; the duct **50.3** reproduces as
the far-field limit; the **brake** is linear (`1/R`) against the duct's steep
one, by a factor of **2.8–3× on a common diameter pair**, and that ratio is
convention-independent; ⇒ **wall heating is not the width lever**, which is
why the implementation is deferred.

⚠ **Do NOT quote:**

1. **"169.3 is right in the mean"** — true on one convention only.
2. **"−3.9 mm"** as the lever — the band spans a sign change.
3. any absolute wall-jet `h` as settled — all of them double under the
   other `c_f` definition.
4. **"the coefficient is why nothing fires at the corner"** — that is the
   40 mm **gate**, measured above.
