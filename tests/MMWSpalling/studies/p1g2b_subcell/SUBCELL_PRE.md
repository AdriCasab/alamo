mean over all five bands +19.4 mm; against the single 96 mm mouth value it
would read +10.7 mm, which is the figure being retired.

⚠ **The collar band is the one convention-sensitive row.** Averaging the
piecewise-linear profile over 10–50 mm gives 91.50 mm; averaging only the
profile's own digitised samples inside that band gives 94.00. The collar is
steep (96→88 over 40 mm), so the two differ by ~2.5 mm there and by <0.5 mm
everywhere else. The integral mean is the declared convention; the sample
mean is carried so the difference is visible rather than buried.

⚠⚠ **Meier's profile is a VISIBLE WIDTH — a LOWER bound on Ø, ±5 mm**
(reference item 10). So every error above is an **upper** bound on the
model's excess, and the ±5 mm exceeds the band-to-band spread of 2.3 mm.
