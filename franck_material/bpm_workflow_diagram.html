Millimetre-wave spallation drilling model — code workflow

One drilling simulation couples wave optics, heat conduction, pore-water thermodynamics, rock mechanics and the dusty plasma the ejecta create, in a two-level time loop: optics refreshed per coupling interval, everything else advanced every heat substep.

Inputs — measured & literature data

Dielectric ε(T)

Lab-measured complex permittivity, 293–1273 K. Basalt (α–β kink @673 K), granite (biotite melt @~1073 K).

Thermal & mechanical

k, ρ, cp; E(T), α(T) with quartz-transition spike; strengths σc, σt with retention f(T); Mohr–Coulomb slope q; damage kinetics n, τ; delamination Gc and buckling coefficient k.

Beam definition

Profile (Gaussian / flat-top / LG01 donut, azimuthal order m), total power, waist, frequency.

In-situ conditions

Formation temperature, vertical stress σv = ρgz, lateral ratio K0 (Kirsch field).

Pore water & cracks

Porosity φ, saturation Sw, drainage factor η, Biot αb, ambient pore pressure p0, crack void fraction φcr,max at D=1.

Purge gas & ejecta

Purge flow (quoted at STP, corrected to downhole p, T), annulus pressure pwell, hole radius, trimodal particle-size distribution (1 µm condensate / 10 µm debris / 3 mm chips), field-enhancement factor β.

CRIM mixing law

1 · Rebuild the dielectric map on the current state

neff = (1−φ−φcr)·nrock(T)  +  φ[Swnfluid(T) + (1−Sw)]  +  φcr,   φcr = D·φcr,max

Volume-weighted √ε over matrix (measured ε(T)), pore fluid — liquid water by Debye relaxation below the local boiling point, steam above, so the optics sees a drying front — and gas-filled damage cracks. Evaluated on the current hole geometry (staircase rock mask).

dust_plasma.py

2 · Dusty-plasma boundary layer above the face

dmi/dt = ρ dV/dt·fi − mi/τi,   τi = Lclear/(ugas − vsettle(di))
εp = 1 − ωp²/[ω(ω + iνc)],   ωp² = nee²/ε0me

Every chip the model removes becomes suspended dust, tracked as two first-order pools — fines and coarse — because they clear at completely different rates. Grains in the beam sit far hotter than the face they came from (absorbed power grows as d³ while cooling area only ever goes as d²), so mm ejecta reach 2000–2400 K at a 700 K face and seed electrons thermionically. The resulting Drude permittivity is written into the gas cells, where the solver can shield and reflect on it — often overdense even before breakdown.

breakdown ignition

3 · Avalanche test → interlock or derate

margin = β·Ebeam / Ebd(p, T, ω),   Ebd = Edc·nrel·√(1 + (ω/νc)²)
ignite when   margin ≥ 1  and  coarse ejecta present in the beam

Ignition is an electron avalanche, not thermal ionisation — observed instantaneous at 670–700 K, with no precursor, coinciding with the onset of spallation rather than with any ionisation threshold. The bulk beam field sits 2–3 decades below breakdown, so what lights it is field enhancement β at sharp mm fragments. The trigger is therefore a state (seeds suspended in the beam), not an event — which is what makes a switch-off time meaningful at all.

5 · Absorbed power — split between rock and plasma

q(r,z) ∝ ε″(T) |E|²,  scaled so  ∫q dV = A · frock · Pbeam,   frock = qrock/(qrock + qplasma)

Reflected fraction R exits; of what remains, only the rock share heats rock. Power taken by the plasma is a genuine shielding loss that never reaches the face. Intensity is averaged between planes, not complex amplitude — averaging the field first loses cos²(Δφ/2) ≈ 0.77 and biases hot cells against cold ones.

HEAT SUBSTEP  ~4–6 ms  × 20–60
two independent controllers

6 · Beam gating u(t) — melt guard × avalanche guard

u = uthermal × uavoid × ginterlock

Thermal (power_control): bang-bang thermostat with a rate-aware margin, OFF at Tlimit − 1.2·Δt·max(q)/ρcp so the peak cannot cross the melt limit inside one substep — or ramp / open-loop pulse.

Plasma (plasma_control): trip stops; interlock cuts the beam at ignition and restarts on a fixed delay, on seed clearance, or on face cooling; avoid prevents the avalanche outright — since margin ∝ E ∝ √(uP), holding a target margin needs exactly uavoid = (margintarget/marginfull)².

Linear optics ⇒ all of this just rescales q, so it runs every 4–6 ms substep at no extra BPM cost. Intervals the interlock keeps fully dark skip the solve entirely.

finite volume

7 · Heat conduction — water-modified bulk properties

(ρc)eff ∂T/∂t = (1/r)∂r(r keff ∂rT) + ∂z(keff ∂zT) + u·q

(ρc)eff: rock + liquid water below boiling, latent-heat spike LρwφSw/ΔT across coexistence; keff blends rock with water / steam by phase state. Adiabatic surface; far boundaries at formation T.

three regimes

8 · Pore pressure p(T)

liquid expansion: p = p0 + ηβw/(cw+cφ)·ΔT  (T < Tboil)
coexistence: p = psat(T), Clausius–Clapeyron  (Tboil ≤ T ≤ Tcrit)
supercritical: p = p(Tcrit)·T/Tcrit  (T > Tcrit)

Joined as p = max(pliq, psat): sealed pores ride the liquid line to supercritical; drained pores follow the boiling curve. Only the thermally induced overpressure Δp = p − p0 acts mechanically (the exposed wall drains the ambient component).

closed-form elasticity

9 · Principal effective stresses in the skin

σ1′ = E(T)α(T)ΔT/(1−ν) − curvature relief + Kirsch tangential + shed load − αbΔp
σ3′ = Kirsch normal confinement − αbΔp

Thermal skin stress with concave/convex curvature correction (capped); biaxial Kirsch field by local surface angle: 3σh−σv at the bottom, 3σv−σh on walls; Terzaghi with the pore overpressure. Load shedding: a cell carries only (1−D) of its load — the deficit Dσ transfers to nearby intact rock over a shear-lag length, conserving force, so a softening skin drives the damage front deeper. The driver stays the ligament stress (for imposed strain the two (1−D) factors cancel); (1−D) enters as load-carrying capacity.

continuum damage

10 · Damage — Mohr–Coulomb with tension cutoff

shear: σ1′ ≥ σc·f(T) + q·max(σ3′, 0)
tension: min(σ1′, σ3′) ≤ −σt·f(T)
dD/dt = (overshearn + overtensn) / τ

One criterion, temperature-degraded by f(T) and confinement-strengthened by q. The tension cutoff on σ3′ is steam spalling; on σ1′ it is tensile splitting (K0 < 1/3). A brittle–ductile warning fires for hot, confined rock (T > 900 K, σ3′ > 50 MPa).

Hutchinson–Suo release

11 · Detachment & spall removal

G = Gss·[1 − (σbuck/σ)²] ≥ Gc,   Gss = (1−ν²)/2E · ∫0tσ²dz,   σbuck = kE/(1−ν²)·(t/a)²

Damage alone does not free a flake: a layer in biaxial compression is clamped, and a closed delamination crack releases nothing until the layer buckles. Gss is the strain energy per unit interface area (the z-integral converts density to energy per unit lateral crack area); the bracket throttles it to zero below the buckling stress. When G reaches Gc the whole damaged layer leaves as one chip — episodic mm-scale release, not continuous thin peeling. Ejecta carry ρcpΔT·V away, the heat sink that self-regulates the face below melting. Rock mask, curvatures and conductivities update.

Sizing the switch-off time — the four recovery clocks

Computed from the purge hydraulics and plasma physics alone (recovery_budget()), so the interlock can be sized without running the coupled model. At 5 kW, 1 MPa, 150 L/s STP in a 10 cm hole they separate by seven orders of magnitude — and the binding one cannot be fixed by waiting.

3.6 × 10−7 s

Electron loss

Attachment on grains, ambipolar diffusion, recombination. The discharge is beam-sustained, so it dies with the field.

never binds
0.025 s

Fines clear

1 and 10 µm modes ride the gas out: Lclear/ugas at u = 4.0 m/s.

cleared by waiting
∞

Coarse ejecta clear

The 3 mm chips that seed ignition settle at 6.9 m/s against a 4.0 m/s annulus — the purge cannot lift them at all.

no off-time fixes this
0.93 s

Rock skin cools

The mm thermal skin decays on L²/κ. The slowest clock — and the one you are spending, not waiting out.

a cost, not a prerequisite

Because the ignition seeds are never lifted, a fixed off-time cools the face for nothing and the beam re-trips on restart. The remedy is hydraulic — raising the annulus velocity above the settling velocity (300 L/s STP → 8.1 m/s clears the coarse mode in 0.089 s) — or continuous margin control, which holds the face hot and never lets the avalanche start.
