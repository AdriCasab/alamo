## Beam column resolution study (run directly, the user's instruction 11-10 evening; no packet)

Why: P2-31's mesh test showed everything under the beam after melt onset moving with the cell, the regime class
included. Question: how small must the cell be against the absorption depth.

What ran: `tests/MMWSpalling/studies/beam_column_1110/` -- P2-25's regime deck cut to a 4 x 4 column under a 100x
wider beam (same absorbed flux, laterally uniform), flaws off, dt 0.0008 s on every leg; six points x six cells x
(model as is, heat only). 68 legs rc 0, four stopped on purpose (the 0.098 mm cell at D 4 and 8 mm). About 55 minutes
wall, six at a time, one rank each (np 1 = np 4 checked on a smoke leg: thermo identical but timers and round-off).

Result (README.md and RESULTS.txt in the study):
- The heat solution converges, second order. dz <= D/4 gives the temperature to 2 %, dz <= D/8 the melt onset to
  2-4 %. At the map's 1.5625 mm cell the melt onset is 131 / 54 / 17 / 7 / 4 % late at D 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 mm.
- The onset criterion fires 22-33 % early when the cell is as large as D: the surface reconstruction extrapolates an
  exponential, the beam's profile is flat at the surface. First-order convergence.
- The thermal length from the top two cells grows as 1/dz under the beam: no converged value. The flake thickness and
  the buckling condition read it, so the class flips with the cell and not one way (D1 Q2: S S S M M S).
- Only "D >= 4 mm melts" holds at every cell.

Corrections to what I told the user the same evening: (1) a beam face DOES get the reconstructed surface temperature
(I had said it read the cell value); the reconstruction is the problem, not its absence. (2) the first heat-only
launch aborted at parse (vapour on with spall off is refused on the face list); re-run with vapour off.

Hiccups: a relative redirect in a background launch failed ("no such file"); absolute paths fixed it. The line scan
is slow while a firing is being withdrawn every step.
