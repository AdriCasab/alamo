## §3 Physical standpoint on entrainment (user asked "no mixture, T marches out?")

A turbulent jet MUST entrain (Ricou–Spalding ṁ/ṁ₀ ≈ 0.32·x/D·√(ρ_a/ρ_j): at 85 mm, 11 nozzle D ⇒
~3× its mass; the model's 1/φ = 1.55 is on the LOW side). In a blind hole the only gas available is
its own recirculating products ⇒ the `exhaust` TOPOLOGY is physically right; "no mixture" is the
limit of that topology with T_rec = T_jet, and is wrong twice (entrainment is unavoidable; the
recirculated gas has lost heat to the floor, the walls and the water-cooled burner face/body). What
the model gets wrong is not the mixing: (i) 1,900 K is treated as a nozzle-EXIT temperature on a
momentum-jet decay, whereas the flame is lifted and burns in flight (chamber sensor ~550 °C), so the
ARRIVING total T ~1,700 K is a combustion result, not a residual; (ii) the recirculated gas returns
with no loss (T_rec = T after the last floor bin). Most correct structure: exhaust-topology
entrainment, a burning-jet decay (or heat release along the jet) anchored to Kant's T_flame(s), and
a loss term on the recirculated gas, T_rec = T_exhaust − Q_body/(ṁ_rec c_p), with the cooling-water
rise as its measurement. Numbers: "no mixture" (φ = 1, m = ṁ_n) gives 1,900 K at the axis and
1,900 − 1,870/(3.78e-3·1,250) = 1,504 K at the ring (same ring T, hotter axis ⇒ +15 % axis flux) —
it moves delivery UP, not down.

## §5 What is wrong with the wall heating (user asked for the explanation; floor-map scaling agreed)

Five layers, from the code and the archive:
1. **Classification.** A side face belongs to a COLUMN; a column is "above the plane" if its top is at
   or above the nozzle exit (s ≤ 0). Under feet the nozzle sits 50 mm above the pad rock, so every
   face of the hole wall alongside the burner body is above-plane — 4,126 columns/quarter in m13.
   Below-plane side faces are only the pit walls (bore) and the pad-ring risers (pads).
2. **The switch.** `side_face_factor_above` (D2r-1 sentinel −1 = inherit `side_face_factor`) is set to
   **0.0** in the CTRL5 argv and carried into feet_argv ⇒ above-plane faces receive 0 × flux. thermo:
   `side_P_face_above` = 0; below-plane: pads 629 W (11.2 cm²), bore 132 W (1.5 cm²), band r ≥ 40
   26 W (1.0 cm²). So "the wall" gets heat only in the 50 mm between pad rock and nozzle, 26 W.
3. **Why it was zeroed.** D2j-0b: an unheated vertical step face drains k(T_top − T_wall)/dx from a
   firing cell into its taller neighbour ⇒ inward cascade, diverging as 1/dx ⇒ D2k added side-face
   flux (default-off) to cure the mesh dependence. D2r-1 showed above-plane SIDE faces close the
   remaining 6/8/14 % mesh gap. BUT D2v M2 (above-plane f = 1, mouth gas 1,690 K) BALLOONED the mouth
   (Ø 137 vs Meier 96 at 10 mm), and D2w/D2x found the side term on the floor CONE FLANKS doubles the
   rate (1.50 → 3.1 m/h; +59 % power) with no wall-band treatment closing the bore without giving the
   rate back. The compromise that shipped: below-plane side flux ON (f via the step-height closure),
   above-plane OFF. D2q-2b's 80 W/m²K cap note: "the wall provably cannot be heated into spalling on
   its own" (h* 88–116 at 1,400–1,750 K for the 121 s dwell) — true for a TYPED 80, not for the derived
   372.
4. **What the wall would see if switched on, as the code stands.** Coefficient: the P2-1 derived law,
   wall-jet below z_fill, duct above (p50 372, max 388 W/m²K) — physically reasonable. Gas
   temperature: `side_T_eff` = `jet_T_exhaust` = **1,504 K for every side face at every height**
   (`:2481`), no cooling up the annulus, no mouth dilution unless `jet_free_surface`. ⇒ the whole wall
   from floor to mouth is heated at the floor-exit temperature: that is the ballooning mechanism, and
   it is a BC error, not a coefficient error. Real exhaust rises through a 2.5–10 mm gap past a
   water-cooled 67 mm body (and the slotted skirt), cooling length ≈ ṁc_p/(h_body π D) ≈ 0.45 m.
5. **Consequence.** Bore = 2·foot_r_outer = 79.3 mm, flat to 0.1 mm from 125 to 400 mm: the tool
   stance, not a prediction. Meier's 85 ± 5 mm needs ~2.5 mm of radial wall spalling at
   q ≈ 372·(1,504 − T_wall) ≈ 0.3 MW/m² — marginal (Kant threshold class), slow, exactly what a
   few-mm widening over minutes looks like — PROVIDED the gas cools with height so the mouth does not
   go first.
⇒ The fix is not "a bigger wall coefficient": it is (a) un-zero the above-plane factor with the
derived law, and (b) give the wall gas a height-decaying temperature (one new site, one parameter
h_body, measurable by the cooling-water rise), with L_c = ∞ as the negative control that must
reproduce M2's ballooning. (c) Only meaningful AFTER the floor map ×0.68, because D2w's rate doubling
came from side heating on top of a 2× floor delivery.
