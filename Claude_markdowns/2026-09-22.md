**Done (working tree, uncommitted; binary `bin/mmwspalling-3d-g++-amr`).**
- `SurfaceOwner(lev)`: the finest level owns the surface. Patch, losses, side faces, beam,
  Sp, clusters, removal, pin/feet/jet state and the CSVs run there only; coarser levels do
  conduction and take the covered region by average-down. `amr.nsubsteps = 1` required.
- `surface.refine_depth` / `surface.refine_height` band tagging on every column's top solid
  cell (moves with recession); `CheckSurfaceCovered` aborts if the surface or the depth-scan
  window leaves the band.
- `RepairRemovalStateAfterRegrid`: cell_cons_interp smears the per-column phi at lateral
  steps on newly refined cells; rebuild the column-linear profile from the exactly copied
  top cell (only cells off by > 1e-6 dz touched, so unchanged grids stay bit-identical).
  Regrid also re-inverts H→T on the remade level. Per-step microstructure refresh skipped
  when mechanics is disabled.
- Build isolation: `make POSTFIX=3d-g++-amr bin/mmwspalling-3d-g++-amr` — the D2k campaign
  binary was never rebuilt.
