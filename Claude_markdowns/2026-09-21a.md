# Meier comparison as it stands (2026-09-21) — the statement D3 can build on

Purpose: close the mesh-gate loop by writing down what is established, with the window,
uncertainties and limitations stated as results. Everything here comes from runs already on
disk; nothing new was run. Audience: the D3 planner and the report writer.

## 1. The configuration this statement is about

- **Support rule (D2f):** burner on three pads, r 28–40 mm, pad height = 0.9-quantile of the pad's
  face heights, stand-off 50 mm, **no mechanical clearance**, **idle clock off**
  (`pinned_idle_cycles` set large per input; the source default is untouched).
- **Gas side (D2c, unchanged):** Martin stagnation h anchored at 1449 W/m²K, J-M nozzle
  temperature 1900 K (adiabatic flame at the measured air/fuel; the thermocouple value is not
  a gas temperature), enthalpy march ring by ring, stagnation decay with entrained mass,
  exhaust recirculation, momentum diameter with core length 8, far-field power law n = 1,
  free-surface dilution, exponential bin update, signed rock-to-gas flux.
- **Rock side:** pinned Robin closure, Weibull flaw a₀ 4/20 µm with V₀ = V_cell, Nasseri
  K_Ic(T), ΔT-driven thermoelastic stress; ε 0.8, h_c 10 W/m²K, T_amb 293 K.
- **Domain:** quarter, 0.12 × 0.12 × 0.40 m; 2 mm (16 ms) and 1 mm (8 ms) meshes;
  binary `6d047b50…`. Runs: `studies/d2i_gate/output/LI0_2mm`, `LI0_1mm`
  (byte-identical to `d2h_openloop/output/I0_*` to 350 s).
- **Not this configuration:** every number from D2b–D2d (they used the clearance clip, which
  D2e showed to be the mesh seed).

## 2. The comparison window and why

**150–350 s.** Both meshes agree there: burner rate 1.491 / 1.461 m/h (150–250 s, −2.1 %) and
1.505 / 1.444 m/h (250–350 s, −4.0 %); whole volume within 8 %; hole diameters within 2 mm at
every depth. After 350 s the 1 mm ring rate falls away (−16 % at 350–450 s, −36 % at 450–550 s;
D2i) for the reason given in §6, so nothing past 350 s is quoted. The first 150 s are the
start-up under the free surface and are excluded as in every earlier step.

Two statements the table supports:
- **Rate:** with every input taken from the experiment, the drawings or a literature correlation
  and nothing tuned, the model drills Meier's rock at Meier's rate to within the mesh and
  support-rule uncertainty. The agreement is on the transient window, not a steady state.
- **Shape and volume:** the model makes the right diameter only in a 25 mm band around 100–125 mm
  depth; above it a funnel, below it a needle pit. The volume excess is that funnel. This is a
  finding about the gas closures, and it is where the physics disagreement with Meier lives.

## 5. Limitations to state as results, not footnotes

1. **The model has no steady drilling state in this configuration, and the long-time ring rate
   is not mesh-converged.** D2g/D2h/D2i removed two real but partial mechanisms (the whole-cell
   clearance clip, seed of D2d's +43 %; the idle clock of the pinned closure, t_cell ∝ dz) and
   D2j-0b (evening, `studies/d2j0b_plane/`) identified the structural one: the per-column surface
   heats and loses only through top faces, so a firing column beside a taller, colder neighbour
   loses k(T_top − T_wall)/dx through an unheated step face into that neighbour's interior
   (≈ 240 kW/m² at 2 mm, 480 at 1 mm, against a pinned budget ≈ 520). Once the step is taller than
   the thermal length (≈ 1.5 mm) the wall is at bulk temperature at any mesh and the loss diverges
   as 1/dx, so the column stalls and becomes the next cold wall — an inward cascade that runs with
   or without the jet, the feet or the exclusion rule, faster at 1 mm. Refinement does not remove
   it; heating the exposed surface does not reach it. (Evidence: `2026-09-20.md`, `2026-09-21.md`,
   D2h/D2i RESULTS, `d2j0b_plane/RESULTS.md`.)
2. **Hole shape:** flux per horizontal area and no side-wall heating produce the needle pit and
   the 80° cone; Meier's spheroid bottom and 5–13 mm wall clearance above the feet both point
   to exhaust heating the wall.
3. **Gas temperature unmeasured;** the sweep must be redone under the current rule.
4. **The jet is supersonic** (Laval, 6 mm throat, 6 bar) and Martin is used outside its range.
5. **0.40 m domain;** the 0.70 m existence question was never run without the clip.

## 6. What D3 may and may not claim

May: an untuned, mesh-checked (to 350 s) drilling rate in agreement with Meier's; a firing
temperature in the spallation band; energy closure; a specific, diagnosable shape disagreement
that localises the model error in the gas closures; and a documented convergence limit with its
cause.
May not: a steady state; a converged long-time rate; a mouth pass or fail; the D2b–D2d numbers
(1.36, 1.65 m/h, the 1436 K sensitivity, the T_nozzle inference); a nozzle-temperature range
until the sweep is redone; any flake-size or size-effect claim (V₀ = V_cell).
