### Implementation takeaways

- **A column τ with a Gaussian evaluated at each cell's depth is not conservative once α varies sideways.** The
  closed form only looked exact because the lateral sum of Pi is P at every depth. Any later α(T) or per-mineral
  absorption on a Gaussian beam inherits this term. It is ≈ (d/z_R)² times the centre-to-edge contrast, so the
  #19 grid's z_R of 80–117 mm makes it ≈ 1 % at onset.
- **Even at a flat α, T1c's spot (ω 20 mm) reads −4.2e-6 on the 0.1 m block**: the Gaussian tail beyond the side
  walls grows with depth. Any closure bar tighter than ≈ 1e-5 on a ω 20 mm leg of that block fails cold.
- **A key added to the rock file reaches every deck that includes it, beam on or off.** Any registration or cost
  guard keyed on a rock property alone moves the face gate through HOS_S and CAL_S.
- The AMReX abort log on a ParmParse exception carries a NUL byte, so `grep` treats it as binary and prints
  nothing. Filter with `perl -ne` or `grep -a`. The probe's `Backtrace.0` was moved out of the repo root, into the
  session scratchpad.
