## Reading

- The model has **no steady drilling state** in this configuration: the wall above the nozzle
  plane receives no flux (`s ≤ 0` rule, D2a2) and is a cold sink; silence propagates inward from
  it and the ring goes out. The 2 mm "steady 1.5 m/h to the bottom" is the same transient,
  earlier in its course. A taller 2 mm domain would very likely show the stall too.
- The speed of that front is mesh-dependent because it is decided by a per-cell threshold next
  to a cold neighbour (edge-cell average over 1 mm is colder than over 2 mm) and by the idle
  timeout (∝ dz). Refinement will not converge it; it is a closure problem at the wall.
- So: rock side converged (D2e P-a 0.1 %; Q09 to 300 s), gas/wall side not, and not by
  refinement.

## Recommended next step (no new pair first)

1. **Read the two end plotfiles** (L09_2mm at 573 s, L09_1mm at 621 s; 1 mm wrote one at the
   stop): T in the top cells across r = 20–50 mm in the pad sectors, the flame fields if saved,

2. **Then one physics change, not a rule tweak: a wall closure.** The exhaust flows up the wall
   in reality (thesis: underream at pauses, thermally altered band); the model's wall is cold by
   fiat. Options: (a) wall columns above the nozzle plane receive the exhaust of the last ring
   (T_exhaust) with a wall-jet h, default-off; (b) at minimum, do not zero the flux at s ≤ 0 but
   decay it. This is D2d §7(h) item 1, and it is also where the tube-wall confinement (D2f
   candidate) lives.
3. **Make the two remaining cell-scale rules physical while there:** idle timeout in seconds or
   in thermal-layer thickness, not in cells; removal increment already in metres via dz/2 but
   note it in the convergence statement.
4. **Change the convergence target.** Do not ask two transients to agree to 5 % to the bottom of
   a 0.40 m box when the system has no fixed point. Ask: does the model reach a steady ring
   rate with the wall closure on, and is *that* rate within 5 % across meshes over a 100 s
   window? One 350 s pair (≈ 1.5 h at 1 mm) per candidate is enough; the split shows by 300 s.

## On "too much mesh work?"
The mesh pairs were the most productive thing since D2b: they retired a false Meier agreement
(the clip), settled the support rule, and now show the model has no steady state. What should
stop is testing *rules* by refinement; what should start is the wall physics, with the pair
method kept as the acceptance test. Budget: one plotfile read (hours of analysis, no runs), one
default-off closure with a unit test, one 350 s pair. If that pair still diverges, the problem
is elsewhere and D3 should say so rather than quote any Meier number.

Push back on three points:
1. **"Idle timeout → face form → cools; 802 vs 26 fits exactly."** Not supported. (a) In the
   face form the cell receives G(T_f − T_c), which for a cell below the pin is *more* heat than
   the pinned flux, not less; unpinning does not cool a column. (b) The idle threshold is
   itself ∝ dz, so counting idle columns with a ruler half as long at 1 mm produces a larger
   count for the same firing pattern; the 8× is partly the ruler. (c) Healthy pad columns fire
   every 1–3 s against thresholds of 8 / 17 s (events, 300 s), so the timer does not act on
   the pad while it is firing; it acts after a column has already slowed. The timer should be
   made physical or removed, but as a *consequence* fix, not the lead.
2. **Zero-code test for the timer exists:** `surface_patch.pinned_idle_cycles` very large (never
   idle) leaves only the state rule T_s = min(T_f, T_pin) that SurfaceCellFlux already applies.
   No new key needed for the test; a new key is needed only if the default is to change.
3. **R1 does not test the "taller box stalls too" claim.** R1 ran with `foot_body_clearance = 1`,
   which cuts proud rim columns mechanically regardless of their temperature, i.e. it removes
   exactly the mechanism under discussion. The claim is *untested*, not contradicted; the test is
   a no-clip 2 mm run in 0.70 m. I withdraw "very likely" to "untested".

Sequencing I would propose (each a 350 s pair unless noted, no code for the first three):
(i) `spall.flake_coherence_length` = 5–10 mm (A2 has it at 0) — tests the edge-criterion
hypothesis from the fields; (ii) open-loop: `nozzle_descent = feed` at the 2 mm rate +
