## Reviewer: D2z (resumed) packet — sound skeleton, five must-fixes before launch

Correct and kept: δ = |n + φ_S − φ_T| from both phases; time-based phase (stored remainder is single-valued under whole-cell); α = diffusivity, not the code's `kappa`; the two area laws settled by legs at both meshes with a common conductance correction; aggregate-window gate at r<25 with W2/W1 as the whole-cell reference; S2f as the f = 0 control with the drain registered (first "key-on at f=0 == key-off" test); S0 identity to RB_D; item 0a re-measures δ on the re-baselined logs; item 0b reports and does not fix; scope to `sp_weibull_spall`.

Must-fix:
1. **The conductance target is cited at the wrong site.** `Removal.H:1441-1452` is the phi RECESSION shift. Lateral conduction is the `k_face` lambda in the conduction kernel, `MMWSpalling.H:~1537-1571` (void test at the `rem(ii,jj,kk) > 0.5` return). g(δ) multiplies the harmonic face conductance there, for lateral axes only, on the face between the SHORTER column's top cell (k = k_top_S) and the TALLER column's cell at the same level (k_top_T > k). The test must be SYMMETRIC (evaluate from both cells with the same per-pair δ) or energy is not conserved. The GB contact term composes in series after it. The implementer's own D2z pre-flight note already said this; the planner did not carry it.
2. **Criterion (b)'s witness does not see the mechanism.** `WallStepDiagnostic` (`:1861-1905`) computes `kc·(Tc − T_nb)/dx` — the RAW conductance, independent of the kernel's face factor. Scaling k_face changes the flux but this witness only moves through ΔT. It must report the EFFECTIVE flux (× g(δ) for the same face rule), ideally raw and effective side by side, or (b) fails correct code. Also decompose the expected ~14×: per-face g at time-weighted δ 0.16–0.20 with a = dz·v/α ≈ 1.29 is g ≈ 0.28 (3.5×), the rest is the step-time population (steps present 8–15 % vs 30–44 % of the time). State both so a failure can be attributed.
3. **The `wetted` law must be the exact ramp, not δ²/2.** `RiserWeight` is (√(dx²+(n dz)²) − dx)/(n dz) — the wetted law is its continuous-δ version, riser area = dy·dx·(√(1+δ²) − 1) for cubic cells. δ²/2 is the small-δ limit only: at δ = 2 it charges 2.0 footprints vs 1.24 true, at δ = 3 4.5 vs 2.16 — unphysical exactly where A's floor lives (53 % of stepped pairs ≥ 2 cells). Same defect for the `step` law's "refuting evidence": on multi-cell risers δ ≈ n charges the FULL vertical face (1.0/cell) where D paid the ramp (0.41–0.72), so on A's cascaded floor SW can draw MORE side power than D without δ being inverted. Restate per population: less than D on the flat interior, possibly more on the cascade.
4. **Say how δ maps onto per-cell faces for n ≥ 2.** The kernel deposits per cell with a void neighbour; T's exposed cells are levels k_top_S+1 … k_top_T. True face height δ = (1 − φ_T) on T's top cell + 1 per middle cell + φ_S on the lowest (the φ_S piece geometrically belongs to T's cell at level k_top_S, which has no void neighbour, so fold it into the lowest exposed cell). For n = 1 the single cell gets δ. Without this the four sites can be "identical" and still disagree with the ledger.
5. **Name the fallback.** With < 2 voids a column has no dt_i: fallback φ = 1 ⇒ δ = n (today's face) and g = 1, counted in `delta_fallback_cols`. The packet counts it but never states the value.
