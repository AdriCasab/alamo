### What the 2× means

The floor flux is q = h·(T_gas − T_s) and recession = q / ρc(T_s − T_0). Two levers, degenerate
on the floor:

* **gas side** — nozzle T unmeasured (1450–1850 K), cooling-water loss unmeasured (up to 5.6 kW
  ⇒ 100–300 K). At the legs' ≈1460 K, halving the floor flux by T_gas alone needs ≈1140 K:
  **below the thesis range**. The gas side cannot do 2× on its own.
* **rock side — the spall temperature.** With T_gas 1464 and T_0 293:
  recession ∝ (T_g − T_s)/(T_s − T_0) = 1.21 at T_s 822 K (the model), 0.78 at 950, 0.66 at 1000,
  0.61 at 1020. **T_s ≈ 1000–1020 K halves the floor rate by itself**, i.e. ΔT_fire ≈ 710–730 K —
  inside Rauenzahn's measured 300–900 °C band (`meier_thesis_notes.md:123`), where the model's
  528 K sits low-middle. And it keeps the wall consistent: FH's widening 7.1 → ≈3.6 mm (Ø ≈ 84),
  FM's 13.7 → ≈6.9 mm (Ø ≈ 87), both in band. ⚠ T_s in the model is set by the Weibull criterion
  with V0 = V_cell (size effect OFF, user's 09-16 choice); the "structural" 1.32–1.37 J/mm³ is
  structural only *given* that T_s. So this is possibly not missing physics but a mis-set spall
  temperature — a hypothesis for ONE run, not a claim.

The wall bracket (h 38–169, 4.4×) is too wide to separate the two levers, and the thesis anchors
neither T_gas nor T_s better than the bands above. Report both as bands.

## 11. Regime switch (spall vs melt/vapour) — design note for after Meier

Removal.H has spall (Weibull K_I ≥ K_Ic(T)), melt_aware (scales driving stress by melt fraction),
vapor (per-phase T_vap_lo/hi, enthalpy). NO gradient / heating-rate / flux condition anywhere ⇒ a
surface heated to 823 K by any route spalls; MMW volumetric heating would spall everything and never
reach melt (as the melt-aware study found). Physics: in-plane stress is local σ = EαΔT/(1−ν), but a
FLAKE needs a thin compressed layer over cold rock (failure plane δ_fp within ~0.2–1 mm of the
surface): buckling/delamination of a compressed film. Gradient enters through δ_fp.
Proposal (ranked): A) minimum surface-gradient gate on the spall criterion, G_min from Kant's
h_min ≈ 500 W/m²K (q ≈ 0.4 MW/m² ⇒ ∂T/∂z ≈ 160 K/mm with k 2.5; δ_fp ≈ 0.6 mm for ΔT_fp 100 K);
evaluate from the column profile (T_top−T_below)/dz or, for surface heating, q/k; default-off key;
⚠ consequence: wall at h 38–169 (q ≈ 0.03–0.12 MW/m²) would NOT spall ⇒ the FH wall result rests on
a mechanism Kant's threshold excludes; the underream must then come from the pad-ring/skirt zone.
E) sub-cell surface T reconstruction T_s ≈ T_cell + q dz/(2k) (633 K at Meier's 0.95 MW/m², dz 2 mm,
k 1.5!) for both the spall and melt checks. B) later: thin-film buckling-delamination criterion
(Hutchinson–Suo) giving flake thickness as output. C) α(T) MMW absorption for localisation.
Validation sets already in repo: Kant SOD spall/no-spall ladder (130–160 mm transitions) = direct
test of G_min; Zhang/Oglesby MMW with spall ON = must not spall.

## 13. D2ad results — interpretation (reviewer)

**Clean negative result with a mechanism.** No (F_q, T_s) cell satisfies both halves of the window:
standoff ≤100 mm is reached by Q5T870 (96.7, plateau) and Q35 (55), but every reduced-flux leg has the
pad ring (r 28–40) receding slower than the 1.5 m/h feed, so the prescribed burner ploughs into it;
three legs aborted on the bore guard at r = 27.9 mm. Only C0 (full flux) clears the ring. Recession by
radius shows it: at C0 the 32–40 mm band trails the axis by 5 %; at Q35 it trails by 60 %.

**What it means.** The two-sided window translates into a requirement on the radial SHAPE of the floor
flux, not its level. Axis: ≤100 mm standoff ⇒ axis recession ≈ feed ⇒ q_axis ≈ e·v = 1.35 J/mm³ ×
0.417 mm/s ≈ 0.56 MW/m². Ring: no contact ⇒ ring recession ≥ feed ⇒ q_ring ≥ 0.56 MW/m². Total
≈ 0.56 × π/4 × 0.085² ≈ 3.2 kW, the energy balance. The present map at C0 delivers ≈1.05 at the axis and
≈0.52 at r = 40 mm (peaked 2:1, Martin's free-jet shape): the ring is already about right, the axis is
2× too hot. Uniform scaling can never fix that; flattening the profile can. Meier's flat/rounded
bottom and calibrated cylinder are the signature of a near-uniform floor flux across the Ø80 bore,
which is what confined impingement in a Ø85 bore with exhaust recirculating through three skirt slots
would produce. **I was wrong on 29 Sep §10 to dismiss radial redistribution from FH's bottom shape:
at full flux everything fires and the shape is hidden; the reduced-flux legs expose it.** D2m (22 Sep)
had already concluded "radial heat distribution is the target".

**On the takeaways.** (i) "s* model retired" is too strong: with the effective R90 ≈ 3.3 the 198·F
predictor lands C0 (180 rising → ~200), Q5 (119 vs 111), Q5T870 (104 vs 97) within 5–10 %; it fails
only once the ring stops (Q35: 86 vs 55). The measured 4.24 m/h is the early undecayed AXIS rate, not
the floor-average that sets the equilibrium — the two numbers in my brief were both right for
