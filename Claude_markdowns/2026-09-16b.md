# 2026-09-16b: Reading of the D2b results (reviewer's conclusion)

Written by the reviewer session after the implementer closed D2b
(`ACTIVE_STEP.md` completion notes, `studies/d2b_feet_rop/RESULTS.md`
§1–5). Nothing here changes code or the packet. It answers one question:
what do the D2b numbers mean, and is the step a failure?

## 1. Short answer

**No. D2b is the first Meier run that reproduces the rate of penetration
without tuning, and the two criteria it fails are one physical effect (the
mouth funnel) and one model-form artefact (no steady pit).** The step did
what its packet asked and should be accepted as a step. As a validation
result it reads: "ROP reproduced within the data's own spread; hole shape
right below the mouth; funnel and steadiness open."

## 3. The two real problems

### 3.1 The mouth funnel

Wall radius from the simulation: Ø 216 mm at 10 mm depth, 174 at 20,
132 at 50, 92 at 100. Below 100 mm the hole is Meier's hole. Above it the
model removes a funnel that accounts for the whole depth-mean-Ø miss and
the whole volume miss.

Why the model makes a funnel: before a hole exists, the wall jet spreads
over the free surface and the enthalpy march keeps the gas at ≈ 1270 K
out to the domain corner (168 mm), because it loses heat only to the rock
and never mixes with ambient air. Exhaust recirculation is also switched
on from the first step, before there is any exhaust to recirculate. Both
make the first 100 s fire rock out to r ≈ 170 mm at 0.3–0.5 m/h.

Whether this is a real miss is not yet known. Meier's 3.42 L over 0.50 m
gives a mean Ø of 93 mm, which is already the top of the stated 85–93
range. If the volume was measured (filling, cuttings) it includes any
entry funnel; if it was computed from a diameter it does not. **Check
Ch. 8 for how the volume and diameter were measured and whether the entry
had a funnel before scoring this row.** Flame-jet holes do usually flare
at the collar.

### 3.2 No steady state

Every exhaust-mode run ended at the bottom watchdog (J-M at 342 s, J-5 at
150 s, J-10 at 103 s). The centre pit runs away because the h expression
clips the stand-off at 12 D: beyond 90 mm the centre keeps 1.86× the
ring's coefficient at any gas temperature, so it can never slow to the
ring's rate. This is an extrapolation choice, not physics. Consequences:

- the flatness row fails on `jet_s_c` drift (0.39 mm/s against 0.02);
- the mesh row is recorded FAIL although its numbers are inside 5 %;
- the ROP is a transient-window value (rising 1.20 → 1.40 m/h as the
  frozen mouth stops taking heat and T_rec climbs 1247 → 1561 K).

The ring feels the pit only through the face power, so the ROP conclusion
survives; the precision claim does not.

## 4. What the sensitivities say

| change from J-M | ROP | reading |
|---|---|---|
| T_nozzle 1900 → 1436 K | 1.36 → 0.67 m/h | ≈ 0.11 %/K; a 1700 K flame gives ≈ 1.05, the band's floor |
| entrainment exhaust → fixed 293 K | stall at 162 s (feet 12 mm down) | as the hand model predicted; where an operator would push |
| h × 3.5 (J-5) / × 7 (J-10) | 2.43 / 2.82 m/h | above the band; d ln ROP / d ln h ≈ 0.4–0.5 |
| mesh 2 → 1 mm | −6.4 % burner, −4.8 % ring | in band either way |

The two unknowns, nozzle gas temperature and stagnation h, have plausible
ranges that straddle the data. Nothing was tuned, and the result lands in
the band with the most defensible values (correlation h at measured flow,
adiabatic T). That is a good validation state for a first-principles
model, but the model cannot yet discriminate inside the band: the pass is
contingent on the flame being close to adiabatic.

## 5. Is there more physics to implement?

Yes, and the results say which. In order of leverage:

1. **h beyond 12 D, or confined-jet stagnation.** Needed for any steady
   pit and therefore for any steady scored window. Model decision, not
   tuning; the hand model with the unclipped Martin shape finds a J-M
   equilibrium at s_c ≈ 0.40 m and 1.93 m/h.
2. **Ambient entrainment into the wall jet on the free surface**, and a
   depth-dependent switch from ambient to exhaust recirculation. This is
   what removes the funnel, if the thesis says there was none.
3. **A nozzle-exit temperature from a burner energy balance** (fuel and
   air flows, dissociation, wall cooling; chamber TC 1436 K uncorrected vs
   1900 K adiabatic). This is the dominant uncertainty on ROP and cannot
   be settled by rock physics.
4. **Shielding of pad-covered rock.** Every ring column currently gets the
   full wall-jet flux under the feet.
5. **Side-wall heating by the rising exhaust and the slope area factor.**
   Walls reach 78–84° with flux on horizontal area only. These widen the
   hole below the mouth, which is the wrong direction for the current
   miss, but they are real.

Not physics, but needed before the next scored run:

- a taller domain (0.5 m) so the pit does not end the run;
- retire the surface-normal arm under the pinned closure (it is inert:
  the cos θ normal thickness is divided back out);
- checkpoint `nozzle_z`, the feet flag and `T_rec`;
- score volume and power inside the hole and diameter by depth range, as
  the addendum already does, once the thesis measurement method is known.

## 6. How to record the verdict

Separate the two verdicts. The **step** verdict is about the packet: code,
unit test 20/20, targeted witnesses identical, pre-registered prediction,
scored as it came out, no tuning. Accept. The **physics** verdict is the
sentence in §1, with the table in §2. Do not carry "4 PASS / 4 FAIL" into
the roadmap; two of the four fails are the missing steady window and one
is the funnel restated.
