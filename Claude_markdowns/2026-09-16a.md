## Findings

1. **Nozzle T at stagnation (v1) makes the centre run away** from any feed:
   2.8–36 m/h. Every 3-D wall then sits at `input_drilling`'s coherence cap
   (63°), and capped cells overheat to T_top ≈ 1500 K. The decay fixes the
   runaway: the centre self-regulates at s_c ≈ 60–90 mm.
2. **Under a prescribed feed with the decay, the flank falls behind the
   feed.** The nozzle plane passes the r = 40 mm ring at 140–220 s; the
   no-flux rule and the cap then freeze everything, and 4 of 9 runs end on
   the nozzle-collision guard at r ≈ 17 mm. The early window (40–110 s) is
   the closure's answer.
3. **Early-window ring ROP at r = 40 mm (1900 K, decay):** JM 0.54, J5 0.57,
   J10 0.71 m/h. That is 2–3× below the reference table, because T_stag is
   1000–1270 K (the centre leads the nozzle), not the 1498 K at SOD.
   - Entrained mass: J10 1.27 m/h, J5 0.62 m/h. Nozzle T: J5 3.19 m/h
     (reference 3.03).
4. **Hole Ø at 10 mm depth, 1900 K:** JM 96, J5 84, J10 88 mm; 1436 K: 71–79
   mm. Predictions 2 and 3 are refuted, and 1 is confirmed.
5. **The reference table's decay rows look computed with D = 7.1 mm** (φ from
   D2a's `jet.py`); the nozzle rows reproduce. The first packet version's
   hole criterion was excess = 528 K.
6. **D2b recommendation:** `jet_stagnation = decay` +
   `jet_entrained_mass = 1` (energy-conserving). The coherence cap vs
   `surface_normal` decision is still open.
