## 3. What the 09-17 findings change

From the thesis reread (page-cited in `meier_thesis_notes.md`):

1. **The nozzle gas temperature is unmeasured.** The igniter thermocouple sat upstream of the
   lifted flame and read ≈ 550 °C while drilling; 1163 °C was the attached-flame peak. Our
   "1436 K lower bound" is void. 1900 K adiabatic is the upper bound; cooling water 160 L/h with
   an unreported ΔT lowers it (10 K ⇒ 1.9 kW ⇒ ≈ 100 K).
2. **Volume must be scored on the whole excavation** (Meier filled the hole with water). D2b
   whole-top 5.68 cm³/s = 2.3× measured. The 09-16 "inside Ø 93" addendum is withdrawn.
3. **The mouth funnel is a real model error** (Fig. 8.8: cylindrical to within a few cm of the
   top). Cause: free-surface wall jet never entrains ambient air; exhaust recirculation from t=0.
4. **The 12 D stand-off clamp** makes the centre pit unbounded; no run reached a steady window;
   the reviewer says 1.36 m/h must not be called the prediction until this is resolved.
5. **Supersonic jet** (Laval, 6 mm throat, choked): Martin is subsonic. Caveat, not a fix.
6. The volume/power "like-for-like" rows, the feet description, and the burner-not-pushed
   wording in the report were corrected; the reference `.md` and RESULTS §0 still carry the old
   statements.

## 4. Recommended next packet: **D2c — Meier retest with a steady state and no funnel**

Principle: two physics changes we can justify from the thesis, one scoring correction, one
sensitivity that replaces a false "measurement", and **one** 3-D campaign. D3 (report rewrite)
after D2c, not before — otherwise D3 documents a transient and a funnel we already know how
to remove.

### 4.2 Physics change 1 — far-field stagnation heating (`jet_h_far`)
- Replace the hard clamp `s ≤ 12 D` with a selectable law beyond 12 D:
  `clamp` (D2b, kept for identity) | `martin` (formula continued) | `power` with exponent n
  (h(0,s) ∝ (12D/s)^n for s > 12 D; n = 1 is the free-jet centreline-velocity scaling, n ≈ 0.5–0.8
  typical stagnation Nu decay). Radial shape unchanged.
- Unit test: identity under `clamp`; monotone decrease; continuity at 12 D.
- **Hand prediction first** (extend `feetmodel.py`): centre equilibrium s_c and the ring ROP
  for each law at 1900 K. Expectation: `martin` gives s_c ≈ 0.4 m (too deep for a 0.3 m box);
  `power` n = 1 gives s_c ≈ 0.1–0.2 m. Pre-register.
- Acceptance is qualitative: **a steady window exists** (jet_s_c drift < 0.02 mm/s for ≥ 150 s)
  for the scored law. Report the s_c and ROP sensitivity to n honestly; n is an extrapolation
  choice, and the packet must say the ring ROP is insensitive to it (through T_rec only) if
  that is what comes out.

### 4.3 Physics change 2 — near-surface entrainment (`jet_T_ent_mode = blend`)
- Physical basis (thesis p. 79: hot free jets dilute strongly; Fig. 8.8: no funnel; collar bore
  Ø 45/56 confines the jet only once the feet are in the hole). Two-part rule, both default-off:
  (a) **stagnation mixing gas**: T_mix = T_amb + (T_exh − T_amb)·f_conf, with
  f_conf = min(1, d_feet / L_conf), L_conf = foot_standoff (50 mm) as the pre-registered
  default, sensitivity 100 mm; (b) **wall-jet dilution on the free surface**: for columns whose
  face is above the hole mouth (or r > r_hole), add ambient entrainment to the march,
  dm/dr = κ_e·2π r·ρ_a·u_wj or the simpler exponential relaxation T_gas → T_amb with a length
  L_e (default 2 D per wall-jet entrainment literature, pre-registered). Keep the budget
  invariant (entrained enthalpy enters at T_amb).
- Unit test: identity when off; conservation; f_conf limits.
- Hand prediction: mouth diameter at 2 and 5 cm depth; expect ≤ 1.2× hole Ø.
- Acceptance: whole-excavation volume rate and depth-mean Ø move onto the measured values or
  the residual is explained; mouth width within the digitised Fig. 8.8 profile ± 10 mm.

### 4.4 Scoring, corrected (write into the packet before runs)
- ROP 1.3–1.6 m/h (band ±20 %), **over a steady window** ≥ 150 s.
- Volume rate **whole excavation** 2.0–3.0 cm³/s; report inside-Ø 93 too.
- Hole shape: r_wall(z) vs the digitised Fig. 8.8 profile (0–0.3 m drilled; extrapolate with the
  run's own v(r) beyond); depth-mean Ø 85–93; minimum ≥ 80 **over the drilled depth** (the
  reviewer's point: the 0.5 m block projection hid a 78 mm minimum). Add a burner-body
  collision at r = 40 mm so the model cannot narrow below the tube.
- ΔT_fire 500–560 K. Removal power inside the hole vs 2.83 kW (not face power).
- Mesh: 1 mm on the **full** domain, not trimmed (exhaust mode couples the rim to T_rec).

### 4.5 The nozzle temperature — a sweep, not a knob
- Runs at T_nozzle = 1600, 1750, 1900 K with the scored law and entrainment. Present
  **ROP(T_nozzle)** and read off the temperature range that reproduces 1.3–1.6 m/h. Stated as an
  inference ("the flame would have to be within X–Y K of adiabatic"), this is legitimate and
  useful; stated as a fit it is not. In parallel, an inquiry item outside the code: ask the
  author for the cooling-water ΔT (160 L/h) or the chamber design heat load; and recompute the
  adiabatic temperature for CH₄/air λ = 1.2 with dissociation to confirm 1900 K.

### 4.6 The 3-D campaign (one, ≈ 8 h)
Quarter domain, 2 mm, 0.12 × 0.12 m; depth 0.30 m if the hand model puts s_c + drilled depth
inside it, else 0.40 m (1.33× cost).
1. J-M, scored law, blend entrainment, 1900 K → steady window, full score. (1 run, ≤ 600 s)
2. Same with `clamp` (D2b identity) and with the alternative far-field law → shows what the
   law does. (2 runs, can stop at steady)
3. T_nozzle 1600, 1750 K. (2 runs)
4. Entrainment off (D2b setting) with the new law → isolates the funnel fix. (1 run)
5. 1 mm full domain, J-M, to steady. (1 run, long; run overnight)
Targeted witnesses per step; full sweep at commit; hand predictions before any run; plotfiles
sparse. No J-5/J-10 (they were 2× too fast and are literature guesses; one sentence in D3).

### 4.7 Out of scope for D2c (record in D3)
Supersonic-jet correction; pad shielding (changes the feet rule: covered rock is undercut, not
heated — a later packet if the ring ROP is still off); side-wall heating and slope area factor;
particle size; 1 MPa clamp; checkpointing of nozzle_z/T_rec (do it if a restart is needed).

### 4.8 What "success" means for D2c
Not "all rows pass". Success = a steady scored window exists; the funnel is gone or explained;
the whole-excavation volume is within the band or its residual is attributed; ROP(T_nozzle) is
reported with the inferred temperature range; the mesh check is on the full domain. Then D3
writes the validation up from a steady state instead of a transient.

- Temperature sweep: state "given J-M, n = 1, free-surface dilution"; check against adiabatic

**Agree with amendments**

  units is not 5 for a supersonic jet. Cold-jet data (Lau/Witze): x_c/D ≈ 4.2 + 1.1 M_j² → ≈ 8

**Scope caution**
