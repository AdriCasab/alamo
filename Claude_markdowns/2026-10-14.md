## §8 — P2-2e proposal: the constraint depth (the stress reference a fixed distance below the surface)

User: "I like the second idea. How do you propose doing it. I think that the temperature differential idea is
important." Advisor proposal, not yet a packet (P2-5 rev 2 is active).

**Form.** At every stress site, replace the stored initial temperature T0(z) with the temperature a fixed
distance d_ref below the point: σ(z) = Eβ[T(z) − T(z + d_ref)]/(1−ν). With the column's near-surface profile
T = T0 + ΔT_s·e^{−z/ℓ}, the surface drive is ΔT_s·(1 − e^{−d_ref/ℓ}). One parameter, d_ref, a physical length
(the constraining rock's reach ≈ the flake's lateral size; Rauenzahn's aspect ratio ~10 × thickness
0.2–1.5 mm ⇒ 2–15 mm).
**Limits:** steep profile (ℓ ≪ d_ref) → the current absolute criterion (Kant level 843.76 K banked; onset flat
with flux — Wilkinson/Höser/Kant); shallow profile (ℓ ≫ d_ref) → drive ≈ d_ref·q/k ∝ FLUX ⇒ steady state fires
iff **q ≥ q_min = k·ΔT_crit/d_ref** — Kant 2016's minimum flux derived, not imposed; uniform slab → 0
(`gradient_blind`); local (no column minimum — rev 6's objection); volumetric heating → drive 1 − e^{−α d_ref}
(reduced, not zero: α 2000/m → 1.00, 100/m → 0.30 — the MMW risk row, physically defensible, unvalidated).
**d_ref from Kant's minimum:** k 2.5 × ΔT_crit 551 / q_min 0.3–0.5e6 = **2.8–4.6 mm ⇒ ~3.5 mm**. Onset vs flux
above the minimum: u(1 − e^{−(q/q_min)/u}) = 1 ⇒ +26 % at 2 q_min, +0.7 % at 5 q_min: flat above ~2 MW/m².
**Drive fractions at 3.5 mm:** Höser axis (ℓ 0.2 mm) 1.00; Kant R500 (0.91) 0.98 ⇒ onset +~10 K; Meier floor
(1.4) 0.92; Kant R150 (3.65) 0.62 and steady d_ref·q/k = 104 K ≪ 551 ⇒ never fires; Meier wall (ℓ 5–9 mm,
q 0.11–0.17) 0.3–0.5 ⇒ never fires. The cap `flake_h_max` 1.5 mm (binding on 95–100 % of wall firings) is the
same rule in disguise and becomes a candidate for retirement.
**⚠⚠ Why P1's `fixed` verdict does not stand.** P1 (10-01) tested a fixed reference depth and rejected it
because R150 and R500 could not be held to the same onset; it chose `driving_ref = flake` (d_ref = c_f·ℓ),
which is SCALE-FREE (drive fraction 0.33 at every flux) and therefore cannot have a minimum flux. R150 runs at
150·(1673 − 844) = **0.12 MW/m², 3× BELOW Kant 2016's minimum** (not on disk until 10-14); R500 at 0.41 sits
on it. The leg P1 demanded to fire is one the experiment says must not. P1 also needed a ×10 flaw refit
(a0 8 → 80 µm) — the drive fraction and the flaw are one knob; d_ref fixed by q_min leaves the flaw at the
banked 8 µm, and the level is re-CHECKED, not refitted.
**⚠ The level coupling to gate first** (roadmap: "keep the level parameter-free"): the Kant anchor at h 500 runs
at ~1.2 q_min where the onset is sensitive in steady state (d_ref·q/k = 574 K vs 551); it fires in the
transient (ℓ_eff 0.91 mm) so the prediction is +10 K — inside 823–903 K. If it is not, d_ref and q_min
disagree and that is the finding.
**Implementation sketch (no code yet):** key `spall.constraint_depth` [m], default 0 = today (identity gate
2639/2639); the four stress sites (lateral criterion, depth scan, Sp_field ×2) read T at z + d_ref from the
gathered column (linear interpolation; it is interior, not the P0 surface problem) — lateral faces along the
