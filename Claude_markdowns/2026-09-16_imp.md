## Problems / things to flag

1. **The hand model finds no centre equilibrium under exhaust
   recirculation** (not anticipated by the packet).
   - As the centre deepens, T_stag decays toward the recirculated exhaust
     temperature, not toward 293 K, and the entrained mass grows without
     bound. So the centre is always faster than the ring, and only the wall
     treatment or the domain bottom limits the central pit.
   - Deep-pit limit: J-M ring ROP **1.82 m/h** (T_rec 1679 K, P_face
     4.1 kW), J-5 4.3, J-10 5.9, J-M at 1436 K 0.97 m/h.
   - With **fixed 293 K entrainment** the model predicts a **stall**: the
     centre drills ahead until its own gas is at T_fire (s_c 114 mm) and the
     ring stops.
   - Stage A's choice (the cap bounds the pit, no cap does not) will
     therefore matter a lot. The watchdog stops a run when the centre is
     40 mm above the bottom.
2. **The packet's freeze-depth formula is off by exactly the stand-off.**
   d(r) = 50·v/(v − v(r)) is the feet's depth when a column freezes; the
   column's own (wall) depth is 50·v(r)/(v − v(r)). The hand model uses the
   latter.
3. **Cost is well above the estimate.**
   - The 60×60×150 domain runs at about 1.9 simulated s per wall minute with
     three 4-rank runs in parallel, so Stage A will end around 19:00.
   - At that rate a 600 s Stage B run is about 5 h. I will apply the packet's
     rule (shorten a run that would exceed about 2 h alone, keeping at least
     150 s of steady window) after the cost probe.
   - Rough finish for B + D + C: between midnight and 03:00. Candidates to
     cut, if you want it sooner:
     - Stage B J-5 / J-10 (bracket only, not scored);
     - the Stage C nozzle-mass run.
4. **`foot_stalled_steps` is nearly useless as a stall signal at 2 mm.** Pad
   heights move in 2 mm steps, so "z_target ≥ previous z_n" is true on most
   steps even while the burner descends normally. It is implemented as the
   packet defines it. The real stall signal is the watchdog's 60 s without
   descent.
5. **The D2a2 cross-check of the hand model is only order-of-magnitude.**
   With D2a2's measured centre stand-offs it gives ring ROPs of 0.39–0.95 m/h
   against D2a2's measured 0.54–1.27 m/h (J-10 is 25–45 % low). The model
   assumes a firing face everywhere; D2a2's face was mostly unfired.
6. **Model limitations already in the packet:**
   - the quarter domain's "tripod" is a 4-fold symmetric proxy;
   - rock under the pads is heated;
   - there is no slope area factor;
   - exhaust recirculation applies from the surface down.
