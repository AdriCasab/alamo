## Hu and Zhang, looked at more closely (user asked; the mesh check stays first)

**Zhang.** Read eqs. (1)–(3), (14), (20), Table 1, Table 4 and §4 directly. Eq. (14) is the bulk power coefficient
only, no reflection. Eq. (20) sets the gas temperature in the losses to 0.86 × the surface temperature — not in our
runs, worth ≈ 8 W of 236 W. A hand estimate with Zhang's printed inputs gives 850–880 / 1070–1130 / 1210–1290 K; our
run with the same inputs 763 / 910 / 993 K; Zhang's Fig. 7 989 / 1400 / 1678 K. So our solver agrees with the hand
estimate to 10–20 % and Zhang's own curve needs 1.25–1.5× more heating than the printed inputs give. The unit of α
(Np/m, an amplitude unit, used in a power formula) is the first thing to check in Oglesby.

**Hu.** Hu's own flux at onset is 0.074 MW/m² (the step part); with the ramp ≈ 0.13 MW/m². Either is 3–6× under the
0.432 MW/m² the buckling condition needs at a 12 mm span. Two gaps, not one: onset level (rock) and buckling (form).
