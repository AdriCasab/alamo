## 1. Why this step, and why now (decision record)

1. **ROP is energy-fixed.** In the Sp = 1 thermostat regime,
   `v = q_abs / (ρ·Cp·ΔT_spall)` (ΔT_spall ≈ 519 K cell-average, Kant-validated).
   Nothing else in the spall model enters to first order. Meier's 1.5 m/h therefore
   measures the absorbed flux (~0.5 MW/m² over the 85 mm hole, ≈ 7 % of 38 kW in
   cuttings) — not spall physics. P4 (debris shielding) is degenerate with delivery
   efficiency and is **retired**.
2. **The Meier discrepancy was a boundary-condition statement.** A prescribed
   30 %-of-38 kW flux forces energy into rock that a flame cannot deliver. The
   physical BC is convective: `q = h·(T_gas − T_s)`; the rock takes only what the
   interface delivers at its own surface temperature. Same law as `losses.h_conv`,
   used as the *source* with T_gas ≈ 1000–1900 K and h ≈ 10 kW/m²K (not a sink
   to 293 K air at 10 W/m²K). Meier's own Ch. 7 values (h = 10 kW/m²K,
   T_ref = 1000 K) with the thermostat give ≈ 3.3 cm³/s vs 2.47 measured,
   uncalibrated. **This is Meier-specific; MMW has no gas side** (its analogues are
   R(T), α(T), and re-radiation at the true surface temperature).
3. **A Robin BC is meaningless at dz = 2 mm.** Boundary layer κ/v ≈ 0.4 mm < dz/2.
   With h = 10 kW/m²K, T_gas = 1000 K:

   | T_s used at the boundary | ⟨q_abs⟩ over a cell's heating cycle | ROP on spot |
   |---|---|---|
   | naive `h·(T_gas − T_cell)` (what `convective_flame` does today) | ~4 MW/m² | ~13 m/h |
   | FV face temperature, `k·(T_face−T_cell)/(dz/2) = h·(T_gas−T_face)`; Bi = h·dz/2k ≈ 6.7 pins T_face ≈ T_gas | ~0.3–0.6 MW/m² | ~1.4 m/h |
   | physical (converged): surface hovers at T_spall ≈ 812 K, thin flakes | 1.9 MW/m² | ~6 m/h |

   Factor 10 between two defensible discretisations on the same mesh. So the
   sub-cell surface state must be resolved / closed **before** any Robin Meier run.
4. **Corollary to check:** at fixed 2.06 MW/m² the cell-average thermostat at 812 K
   implies a *true* surface ≈ 1 400 K (2q√(t/πkρCp) over the ~1.2 s cell heating
   time; equivalently ΔT_cell ≈ 0.39·ΔT_s for the steady exponential layer). P1's
   "melt is unreachable because of the thermostat" may be a cell-average artefact.

**Agreed sequence:** leak fix → **this study** → sub-cell surface closure (if
indicated) → Robin Meier 2D/3D → MMW pivot (D1 α(T)/R(T), D2 known-power anchor).

Robin form to carry into Meier 2D/3D.

## 3. Setup

- Geometry: true 1-D column, `amr.n_cell = 1 1 N` (or the minimum x,y the code
  accepts), Neumann sides, `amr.max_level = 0`.
- Depth: enough that the run never reaches the bottom in the scoring window
  (P1's melt demo was bottom-confounded: `k_top == scan_kbot` forces `h_col = 0`).
  At ≤ 15 m/h over a 40 s window that is ≥ 170 mm + margin → use ≥ 250 mm.
- dz sweep: **2, 1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.125 mm** (N = 125 … 2000). Explicit stepping is
  fine in 1-D (dz²/2κ = 11 ms at 0.125 mm); keep `timestep` ≤ 1 ms for all so
  the removal cadence is comparable.
- Material: Grimsel one-phase set from `input_2d_dev` (k = 1.5, ρ = 2750,
  Cp = 790, E = 30 GPa, ν = 0.3, β = 8e-6, Nasseri K_Ic, a₀ = 20 µm).
- Criterion: `spallation.model = sp_weibull`, `spall.stress_source =
  local_thermoelastic`, `spall.sample = top_cell`, `spall.surface_normal` off
  (flat), `confining.p = 1e6`, `spall.melt_aware = 1` (so Q3 is observable via
  removal behaviour, not just Λ_L).
- **Flaw length fixed across the sweep.** `weibull.V0` rescales a₀ with cell
  volume (`vol_factor = (V_cell/V0)^(1/m)`, MMWSpalling.H ~3177). For a
  resolution study either disable Weibull (`weibull.enabled = 0`, scalar a₀) or
  set V0 = V_cell per input so `vol_factor = 1`. State which in the input header.
- Losses: radiation on (ε = 0.8) — it becomes non-negligible at true-surface T.

## 5. Implementation notes (for the planner to verify and turn into deliverables)

1. **Surface-following Robin source.** `surface_patch.mode = convective_flame`
   applies at `k == sp_topk = domain.bigEnd(2)` (MMWSpalling.H ~848/~910 in
   `AdvanceMaterial`, ~1142/~1232 in `AdvanceMicrostructure`) — the fixed +z face.
   It must apply at the **current top solid cell**. `surface_mf`
   (`UpdateSurfaceMaskFromPhi`: mask = φ ∈ [0, dz)) already follows recession
   and is what `losses.*` uses; use it (or `k_top_col`) for the patch too.
   Keep the change on the microstructure path only (the only removal-aware path).
2. **Two Robin discretisations behind a switch** (e.g. `surface_patch.robin_form
   = cell | face`):
   - cell: `q = h·(T_gas − T_c)` (today's `sp_hfl*(sp_Tfl − Tc)*inv_dz`).
   - face: `T_f = (h·T_gas + (2k/dz)·T_c)/(h + 2k/dz)`, `q = h·(T_gas − T_f)`.
   Default = cell so every existing test stays byte-identical.
3. **Energy ledger for a Robin source.** With Robin there is no fixed incident
   power. Extend the E1 closure counter (`BeamEnergyBalance`, thermo.dat) with
   an "absorbed at interface" accumulator `Σ q_abs·A·dt`; closure = stored +
   frozen-void + radiated vs ∫absorbed. Report per interval, not just cumulative.
4. **Sp gate follows the surface?** Memory notes say some paths read the
   absolute domain-top cell; confirm the gate in `UpdateSpAfterMechanics` and the
   depth scan (`Removal.H` ~153 `k_top_local`) both use the receding top in this
   configuration. A stale gate would silently freeze the column.
5. **Scan cost.** `spall.h_spall_max = 0.015` is 120 cells at 0.125 mm;
   check `spallation.sp.n_segments` and the K_I scan wall time.
6. **Void cells keep frozen H** (Removal.H ~1265) — the closure script relies on
   it; do not change that behaviour.
7. All new keys default-off; witnesses (Kant, Rossi, Hu, `spall_event`,
   `regime_low_high_power`, base `input_2d_dev`) byte-identical.

## 6. Outputs — one table per case, rows = dz

Per run, over a steady window (state it, e.g. last 60 % of the run):
- ROP (mean, and first-half vs second-half to expose drift);
- top-cell T at firing (mean, spread) and the *implied* true-surface T
  (from the flux and an erfc/exponential reconstruction — document the formula);
- flake-thickness distribution (`h_col` events: median, p10, p90, in cells and mm);
- max T anywhere; whether Λ_L > 0 ever appears and where (surface vs bottom);
- energy closure per interval (must hold at every dz — that is the only pass/fail).
- For R-naive vs R-face: ⟨q_abs⟩ over the window (the number that actually
  differs between forms).

Plus one convergence figure per quantity is fine, but the tables are the deliverable.
