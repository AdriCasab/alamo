Where the thermal-spallation model stands

A reviewer's account of the P-series, from the surface-temperature reconstruction (P0) to the lateral flake sink (P1g-2): what each step established, the findings that changed the design, the six things now in the way, the tests that would clear them, and what is and is not quotable against Meier.

1

The goal and the benchmark

The model is meant to be a physically correct, mesh-independent description of thermal spallation that reproduces Meier's 2017 flame-jet drilling experiment on Grimsel (Central Aare) granite, and that works unchanged for millimetre-wave heating. Meier's run is the benchmark because it is fully instrumented: a prescribed feed of 1.5 m/h, a bore of 85 to 96 mm diameter, a standoff that his own figures cap at about 100 mm, a thermal budget, and a cuttings size distribution with an average under 100 µm and a 90th percentile under 300 µm.

The programme runs as a three-phase loop (plan, implement, verify) driven by separate agents. This document is the independent reviewer's reading of the results, not the implementer's or the planner's.

Heat in

A Martin wall-jet coefficient map h(r, s), radially confined to the burner footprint, with a jet enthalpy march in radial bins so the gas cools as it gives up heat. Side faces of steps are heated through an explicit coefficient (169.3 W m⁻² K⁻¹ on Meier runs) with a staircase area correction so a round wall on a square grid gets the right area.

Heat in the rock

Enthalpy conduction with Grimsel properties (k 1.5 W m⁻¹ K⁻¹, ρc 2.17 MJ m⁻³ K⁻¹, α 6.9×10⁻⁷ m² s⁻¹). The surface temperature is reconstructed from the top two cells assuming an exponential skin, and the Robin boundary condition is applied at that reconstructed temperature.

Removal

A linear-elastic fracture criterion on a per-cell Weibull flaw (8 µm scale, modulus 20) against the Nasseri toughness table. The thermoelastic stress is the absolute surface stress on the control configuration; a differential form (stress jump across the flake) was adopted in P1 and is now in question. Flake thickness is 0.4 of the skin, capped at 1.5 mm.

Geometry

Vertical recession is sub-cell: the column's enthalpy profile is shifted by the flake thickness. Lateral recession on open side faces was whole-cell until this week; it now banks the earned thickness per column (P1g-1) and shifts the row of rock behind the face (P1g-2). The rock is one phase and one grain.

4

Four findings that changed the design

A per-column criterion cannot be round on a square grid

A 45° wall carries the same arc with √2 fewer columns, so any criterion that scores one column at a time sees √2 more heat per column on the diagonals. P1b, P1c and P1e all ran into this from different sides. The lateral face criterion removed the bias from the lateral mode: once the Newton length and the coefficient matched the kernel, the sector spread of lateral firing temperature was 3.8 K. The vertical mode still carries it, and it is the residual behind the deep diagonal deficit.

One function call, five mismatches

The lateral criterion calls the same face-flux routine the kernel does. Over three steps it was found to pass a different unit (Pa versus MPa), a different conduction length (the slanted normal distance instead of the cell width), a different coefficient (capped unconditionally where the kernel caps conditionally), a scope test with one of the kernel's two conditions, and a scalar conductivity where the kernel reads a field. Each was documented in a nearby comment and each shipped. The fix was a lock test that parses all four lateral call sites by argument position, strips comments, and fails on any undeclared difference. The verify phase now carries a diff-review checklist built from exactly these classes.

The flake cap is a wall-band effect, and it is the same population three times

About 60 % of the removed volume comes from the capped bands, so the cap sits under the hole's diameter and volume, not under the descent rate. The columns the cap governs are side-heated columns with a flat vertical profile. They are the columns that froze under the differential form in P1 (78 % multi-sided, stuck at 48 mm depth) and the columns the lateral face criterion was built to read. The cap, the P1 collision and the lateral mode are one problem.

The flake thickness has a statistical derivation, and the model's constant is at its edge

Rauenzahn, Walsh and the Meier reference all derive the spall thickness from Weibull statistics: with stress decaying into the rock and failure probability rising as stress to the power m, the median flake is 2.3 to 2.7 skin lengths over m for the cone-shaped stressed volume the literature assumes. The model's 0.4 corresponds to an m near 7; the spallation literature fits 8 to 17 and our own input carries 20. Three Grimsel measurements of spall size (Höser's 200 µm average, Rossi's 250 µm population, Meier's 90th percentile under 300 µm) bracket 0.2 to 0.3 mm, which with Walsh's near-equant aspect ratio at that size means a thickness of 0.1 to 0.25 mm and an m of roughly 15 to 30. The same four sources drive the crack with the absolute surface stress, not the stress jump across the flake.

Two caveats stand on this finding. None of the new sources is in the repository yet, so by the programme's own rule the numbers are unbacked until Höser, Rossi 2020, Shen 2018 and Wilkinson 1993 land under tests/MMWSpalling/validation/ with figure citations. And the modulus must be fitted from onset or surface-temperature data, from Kant's 21 Grimsel legs or from Meier's own run, not chosen to land in the bracket.

What is actually in the way: six bottlenecks

Each one below says what is stuck, why, what unblocks it, and how we would know it is cleared. They are ordered by dependency, not by size. The diagram at the end shows how they chain.

1. A side-heated column has no vertical gradient to read

What is stuck
Everything outside r ≈ 30 mm, which is about 60 % of the removed volume. The flake thickness there is a constant (1.5 mm), not a result. The hole is 10 to 12 mm wider than Meier's at every depth, and whether it ends at 97 or 85 mm is decided by how fast this band recedes, so the width is not a prediction until the constant goes.
Why
The vertical criterion reconstructs the skin from the top two cells of a column. On a wall heated through its side, those two cells are nearly the same temperature, the read returns 4 to 12 mm (Figure 5b), and the cap takes over. The same columns froze under the differential form in P1 because a flat read gives zero drive.
What unblocks
Two things, both now partly built. The lateral face mode reads the horizontal gradient these columns do have, and since P1g-2 it recedes sub-cell. A thickness law that needs no vertical read: skin from the surface flux (ℓ_q = k(T_s − T₀)/q) and thickness from the Weibull integral, 2.3 to 2.7 ℓ/m.
Cleared when
With the lateral mode on at scale, the cap share at r ≥ 40 mm falls from 100 % toward zero, the flake thickness on the wall becomes a distribution, and the volume excess over the control moves. Tests 1, 2 and 7 below.

2. Two definitions of the driving stress, and a flaw size that moves with them

What is stuck
P1g-3 and every step after it. The planner cannot write a thickness law or a flaw refit without knowing which stress the crack sees.
Why
The control uses the absolute surface stress. P1 switched to the stress jump across the flake and refit the flaw from 8 to 80 µm to compensate; the two changes multiply to 2.46 × 0.402 ≈ 1, so they cancel structurally and are one calibration. The differential form has no external source behind it and needs thick flakes to have any drive. It is a physics decision, and it is the user's.
What unblocks
A decision, not a run. The external sources all drive the crack with the absolute stress; the soaked-wall problem that motivated P1 is now served by the lateral mode instead.
Cleared when
The form is chosen and the flaw size re-anchored on Kant's onset legs in the same step. If differential is kept, the P1 freeze population must be shown to fire laterally rather than freeze (Test 8).

3. The spall temperature is a fixed number, and it is the wrong fixed number

What is stuck
P2, the gas-side flux calibration. If it runs now it will tune the jet to compensate for a rock-side error.
Why
The model removes rock at a cell temperature near 756 K regardless of flux, set by the 8 µm flaw and the toughness table. Kant measured 823 K for Grimsel, about 14 % on the rate. The literature also makes the spall temperature rise weakly with flux, because a thinner skin stresses less rock: at m ≥ 15 that is 3 to 4 % per doubling, small, but the level is the lever.
What unblocks
Fitting m from Kant's 21 onset legs, which is the only route to the rock's own modulus rather than an input's. The stressed-volume Weibull then gives both the level and the flux dependence from one parameter, and the same m sets the flake thickness.
Cleared when
The model's onset temperature against flux reproduces Kant's legs, and Wilkinson's 920 to 1100 K at 1 to 1.5 MW/m² on Barre is reproduced as an external check. Tests 3 and 6.

4. The jet puts twice the flux on the floor that Meier's feed allows

What is stuck
The rate and standoff comparison. The model drills Meier's feed only by retreating to a standoff of 170 to 180 mm, against his ceiling of about 100 mm.
Why
At Meier's standoff the floor flux is 2.0 to 2.2 times what his kinematics imply. The September ladder showed the two obvious levers (flux level and feed) have no window: cutting the level starves the pad ring and shrinks the fired radius rather than the depth. The binding term is the radial shape of the coefficient map across 28 to 40 mm, not its level.
What unblocks
Nothing on the rock side, which is why it is parked. It is deliberately queued behind bottlenecks 1 to 3, because the volume comparison it feeds is 60 % cap-governed and the spall temperature it would calibrate against is not yet the rock's.
Cleared when
A pinned-feed leg at 1.5 m/h settles at a standoff of 100 mm or less with the rock side fixed, and the per-sector bore sits in 85 to 96 mm over 150 to 450 mm depth. Tests 9 and 10.

5. Sub-cell lateral recession has not been shown to do the one thing it exists for

What is stuck
The claim that the lateral mode is mesh-independent. The whole point of banking and shifting was to stop a 2 mm cell being voided for a 1.5 mm flake, which would make the wall recession scale with the mesh.
Why
The unit fixture is 60 s long with seven voids per column on rows of four to six cells. P1g-2 shows the shift delays the first void by one re-heat (4 to 7 s) and that after 60 s the shifting group has removed 95 % of what whole-cell removal did (Figure 7). That cannot separate "delayed onset, same rate" from "lower rate", and it was run on one mesh. The vertical mode also still carries its own diagonal deficit at depth (+93 % at 200 to 300 mm), which only a shape leg will read.
What unblocks
A longer fixture leg on a deeper row at 2 mm and 1 mm, scoring cumulative recession depth against time. The row width already permits it.
Cleared when
The recession-depth curves at the two meshes agree within a stated bar after onset, and the per-track accumulator conflation seen in the burst of consecutive-step voids is measured rather than noted. Tests 4 and 5.

6. Nothing since P0b is quotable, and every Meier leg is expensive

What is stuck
Any sentence of the form "the model gives Meier X". None can be written today.
Why
Every P-series run since P0b has used a configuration (absolute form, 8 µm flaw, 1.5 mm cap) that bottlenecks 1 to 3 are about to change twice. A Meier leg is a quarter domain of 60 × 60 × 300 cells run for 500 s in 31,250 steps on four ranks, and the toolchain is not byte-reproducible, so a lost output cannot be regenerated. Several such legs were spent this week finding mismatches in one function call.
What unblocks
Order. Decide the form, land the thickness law and the fitted modulus, then run one Leg B and one Leg A under the final configuration, archived before any harness can delete them.
Cleared when
A results file for a leg that passed its own gates says a Meier number is quotable, per sector, over 150 to 450 mm.

Where the Meier comparison stands

No rate, volume, width or standoff is quotable from P1 through P1g-2. Every P-series run since P0b has used the absolute form with the 8 µm flaw and the 1.5 mm cap, a configuration the programme is about to change, and the lateral runs were unit tests or control-plus-two-keys legs, not Meier comparisons.

Settled on the jet side

  • The mouth: 144 mm → 100 mm once the coefficient map was radially confined. Within 5 mm of Meier at all depths on that leg.
  • The step-face cascade that made results mesh-dependent is removed by heating the side faces.
  • Roundness under the control configuration: 1.003 to 1.019.

The live error

  • Floor flux 2.0 to 2.2 times what Meier's kinematics imply at his standoff.
  • Equilibrium standoff 170 to 180 mm against a ceiling of about 100 mm.
  • Self-paced, the hole is 10 to 12 mm wider than Meier's at every depth below the mouth (98 vs 86 mm at 150 to 200 mm, 97 vs 85 at 200 to 300) and descends at about 3.2 m/h, twice his feed. The extra width is the capped wall band. At Meier's feed the width comes out near his only because the burner sits at twice his standoff.

Healthy

  • The floor interior: decay length resolved, cap never binds, flake 0.55 mm.
  • Energy ledger identical to the control on every lateral leg (1.45×10⁻⁴ relative).
  • Identity: 0 of 2,639 frozen files differ with new keys off, on every step.

Not yet tested

  • Mesh independence of sub-cell lateral recession (the reason it exists).
  • The lateral mode at scale under the consistent coefficient.
  • The spall temperature's level: 756 K in the model against Kant's 823 K for Grimsel, about 14 % on the rate.
7

Open decisions

  1. The criterion form. Return to the absolute surface stress, which the control runs and all four external sources use, or keep the differential form adopted in P1, which needs thick flakes to have any drive and froze soaked columns. This is a physics decision and it depends on nothing else in the queue.
  2. The flake thickness law. Replace 0.4 of the skin capped at 1.5 mm with 2.3 to 2.7 skin lengths over m, with the skin taken from the surface flux rather than two cell temperatures. This removes a cap and a constant and ties thickness to the flaw statistics already in the input.
  3. Where m comes from. Fit it to Kant's onset legs or to Meier's run by Lyu's method. The thickness is then a prediction, and Rossi's second spall population near 1 mm is a prediction about the slowly receding wall that cannot be reached by fitting.
  4. The spall temperature level before the gas side. P2, the flux-level calibration, must not absorb a rock-side temperature error into the jet. The flux dependence of the spall temperature is weak at m of 15 or more (3 to 4 % per doubling); the level is the lever.

Process lessons the programme now carries as rules

  • Diagnose with the field, score with the rate. Event-log variables are conditioned on firing and can invert a comparison; three readings of one bad statistic once gave three different stories.
  • A quantity that decides a branch must be an output, in stated units. The unit bug was invisible because the ratio it inflated was never logged.
  • Firing assertions are two-sided. A lower bound cannot detect over-firing; a quantity bounded by the mechanism's own action cannot be its discriminator.
  • Never hard-code a quantity the run logs. A gas temperature of 1200 K typed into a scoring script reversed a conclusion that the logged triple inverted exactly.
  • A cited artefact that is not in the repository is not evidence. Scoring scripts are code.
  • A re-specified criterion must test the same claim. P1g-1's continuity gate was re-specified to assert the absence of the sink the step was meant to build.
  • Archive before any harness deletes its own output; two pairs of legs were lost that way on a non-reproducible toolchain.
  • The verify phase reads the diff with a checklist and writes a table: duplication of existing code, consistency across sibling call sites, units at comparisons, dead keys, relaxed checks, new tests red on the pre-fix binary.
