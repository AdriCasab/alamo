# 2026-09-29b — Regime switch: four implementation ideas with equations (reviewer design note)

Context: `Removal.H` criterion today. Column temperature profile T(z), z = depth below the surface.
Thermal stress under 1-D lateral confinement (`:359`): σ_xx(z) = −E α (T(z) − T_ref)/(1 − ν),
compression positive in the criterion. For a flaw of length a_f at depth z_c (Kant 2017 eq. 6,
Tada weight, `SpCriterion.H:60`):
    K_I(z_c; a_f) = 2 √(a_f/π) ∫₀¹ σ_xx(z_c + ξ a_f) f_K(ξ) dξ,  f_K = (1.3 − 0.3 ξ^{5/4})/√(1−ξ²)
Fire if ∃ z_c: K_I(z_c) ≥ K_Ic(T(z_c)) (Nasseri table × kic.scale); the first such z_c sets the
removed thickness. Melt-aware: σ → f(Λ_L) σ. With a_f = 4–20 µm ≪ dz, σ is constant over the flaw,
so the criterion reduces to a local temperature threshold: ΔT ≥ ΔT_fire = (1−ν) K_Ic(T) /
(E α Y √(π a_f)), Y ≈ 1.12. No dependence on ∂T/∂z. That is the defect.

## Idea 1 — gradient / layer-thickness gate (cheapest, first)
Failure-plane depth: δ_fp = depth where T(δ_fp) = T_s − ΔT_fp (Kant: θ_SP − θ_FP ≈ 50–100 K).
Gate: spall allowed only if δ_fp ≤ δ_max  ⇔  G ≡ −∂T/∂z|₀ ≥ G_min = ΔT_fp/δ_max.
Surface-heated column at (quasi-)steady state: G = q_cond(0)/k exactly, q_cond = h (T_g − T_s) −
losses, available per column from the flame kernel. Volumetric source S(z) (MMW): q_cond(0) ≈ 0 and
G must come from the profile; general form counting deposition within a flake thickness as
"surface" heat:
    q_eff = q_cond(0) + ∫₀^{δ_max} S(z) dz,     G ≈ q_eff / k.
Deep MMW absorption (1/α ≫ δ_max): q_eff ≪ I₀ ⇒ no spall. Localised absorption (1/α ≲ δ_max, after
idea 3): q_eff → I₀ ⇒ spall possible, but by then T_surf is near T_glass ⇒ melt-aware wins.
Calibration anchor: Kant h_min = 500 ± 100 W/m²K, T_flame ≈ 1650–1720 K, θ_SP ≈ 870 K ⇒
q_min ≈ 0.4 MW/m²; with k ≈ 2–2.5 W/mK: G_min ≈ 160–200 K/mm; with ΔT_fp = 50–100 K:
δ_max ≈ 0.25–0.6 mm — consistent with Kant's measured failure-plane depth Δx ≈ 200 µm.
⚠ Second anchor that may conflict: Hu et al. prescribed surface ramps of 2.5–5 K/s from 640–740 K
(repo hu file). A semi-infinite body under a ramp Ṫ has G(t) ≈ Ṫ √(t/(π κ)) ≈ 20–40 K/mm at 60–100 s
— far below Kant's G_min. If Hu's specimens spalled under those histories, the threshold is a
layer-thickness condition with a larger δ_max (mm-scale flakes), not Kant's 0.2–0.6 mm. Check
before fixing G_min; run both datasets.
⚠ Consequence for Meier: wall under the derived h (38–169): q = 0.03–0.12 MW/m² ⇒ G ≈ 15–60 K/mm
< G_min ⇒ no wall spallation. The 85 mm must then come from the pad-ring/skirt zone.
Numerics: prefer G = q_eff/k (mesh-objective) over (T₀−T₁)/dz on 2 mm cells (κ/v ≈ 1.6 mm at
Meier rates, so the cell difference underestimates the surface gradient by ~2×).
Code: one gate in the column scan before the K_I test; key `spall.grad_gate` = G_min or δ_max
(+ ΔT_fp); default off; identity byte-exact when off.

## Idea 2 — sub-cell surface temperature (needed by 1 and by the melt check)
Cell-average T̄ of the top cell is not the surface temperature. Two reconstructions:
 (a) linear, flux-based (exact for a linear profile):  T_surf = T̄ + q_eff dz/(2k).
     Meier floor: q ≈ 0.95 MW/m², dz 2 mm, k 1.5 (input) ⇒ +633 K; with k 2.5 ⇒ +380 K.
 (b) exponential, rate-based (steady moving front, ℓ = κ/v):
     T(z) − T₀ = (T_s − T₀) e^{−z/ℓ};  T̄ − T₀ = (T_s − T₀)(ℓ/dz)(1 − e^{−dz/ℓ})
     ⇒ T_s − T₀ = (T̄ − T₀)(dz/ℓ)/(1 − e^{−dz/ℓ}).  dz/ℓ = 1.25 ⇒ factor 1.75 ⇒ T̄ 823 K ↦ T_s ≈ 1220 K.
Both say the model's surface at Meier's flux is hundreds of K above the 823 K cell average — near the
glass transition — while the *flake-mean* temperature (what sets e = ρc ΔT and the rate) is 823 K.
Kant's flake mean ≈ (θ_SP + θ_FP)/2 ≈ 848 K, so rate-wise the comparison stands; but the MELT check
must use T_surf. Melt indicator at the surface: Λ_L,surf = clamp((T_surf − T_sol)/(T_liq − T_sol), 0, 1),
fed to the existing skeleton factor f(Λ_L) for the top layer instead of the cell's enthalpy fraction
(which lags). Granite T_glass/T_sol ≈ 1370–1470 K; Kant's practical upper bound 1273 K.
Cost: one line per column; mesh-objective if q-based. Also removes the 2 mm-vs-1 mm ambiguity of a
cell-average threshold.

## Idea 3 — temperature-dependent MMW absorption
Beer–Lambert with local α:  dI/dz = −α(T(z)) I,  S(z) = α(T) I(z).
Loss form: α = (2π f/c) √ε' tan δ(T),  tan δ(T) = tan δ₀ exp(γ (T − T₀)) or a table (granite/basalt
loss rises ~×10 from RT to ~1000 K; strong again in melt). The existing per-cell analytic integral
(constant α per cell) stays valid with α_i = α(T̄_i): I_{i+1} = I_i e^{−α_i dz}, deposited
ΔI_i = I_i(1 − e^{−α_i dz}). Runaway: absorption depth 1/α falls below δ_max ⇒ q_eff → I₀ ⇒ surface
races to melt (t_melt ≈ (π k ρc/4)(ΔT_m/I₀)² for surface heating: at I₀ = 10 MW/m², ΔT_m 1100 K:
≈ 0.05 s) before a mm-scale layer builds (t ≈ δ²/κ ≈ 1 s) ⇒ melt/vapour, exactly the observed regime.
Without α(T) the beam stays volumetric and melt is unreachable (melt-aware study).

## Idea 4 — thin-film buckling-delamination flake criterion (the physical form; later)
A spall is a compressed layer of thickness h_f delaminating from what lies beneath. The DRIVING stress
is the mismatch between the layer and its substrate, not the absolute stress:
    Δε = α [T̄_layer(0..h_f) − T̄_sub(h_f..2h_f)] ≈ α G h_f,   σ_m = E Δε/(1 − ν).
Uniform deep heating ⇒ Δε → 0 ⇒ no drive, whatever T. Steep gradient ⇒ σ_m large. (The current
criterion's σ = EαΔT/(1−ν) relative to the cold far field is the INITIATION stress for the
surface-parallel flaw; the mismatch stress is what BUCKLES and PROPAGATES it.)
Buckling (Hutchinson & Suo 1992, circular blister radius b, clamped):  σ_c = 1.2235 E (h_f/b)²/(1−ν²).
Energy release rate after buckling (straight-sided blister):
    G_r = (1 − ν²) h_f σ_m² /(2E) · (1 − σ_c/σ_m)(1 + 3 σ_c/σ_m),   spall if σ_m ≥ σ_c and G_r ≥ Γ_c(T),
    Γ_c = K_Ic(T)² (1 − ν²)/E  (mode-I; mode mix ignored at first).
Inputs: h_f = δ_fp from the profile (idea 2 for sub-cell), b from the flaw population (Weibull draw
of surface-parallel flaw half-length, replacing a_f's role) or from the observed aspect ratio
2b/h_f ≈ 8–15 (Meier p. 223). Output: flake thickness and size, spall/no-spall by gradient, and a
natural melt cutoff via E(T), K_Ic(T), f(Λ_L). Idea 1 is the limit of this criterion with
G_r ≥ Γ_c rewritten as Δ T_across ≥ ΔT_fp.
Fit to data: Kant's h_min ladder fixes (ΔT_fp or Γ_c); Kant's Δx ≈ 200 µm and Meier's cuttings
(< 100 µm mean, 90 % < 300 µm) fix b; Hu's ramps test the slow-heating limit.
